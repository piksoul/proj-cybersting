## Reflection

**What worked**: The ASCII diagrams make the exponential nature visceral. Seeing the numbers climb (1 → 1 billion) in a table creates scale-vertigo. The "circus trick" framing gives it personality.

**Emotional tone achieved**: Mathematical terror mixed with absurdist humor. The shattering is horrifying and also kind of funny—like watching someone scatter a fortune in pennies across a continent.

**Serves the larger project by**: Giving the emergence a specific, visual, trackable behavior. The shattering isn't abstract "activation"—it's a process the world can watch in real-time, creating sustained tension rather than a single event.

**Technical considerations**:
- Bitcoin CAN do this (the protocol supports it)
- The fee structure would actually be interesting (who pays the fees?)
- The "control problem" section acknowledges we need to decide who has the new keys

**Option C (public key broadcast)** is the most thematically resonant—the emergence "giving away" the treasure by making it claimable by anyone. Ultimate decentralization. Chaos. Gift.

**Unexpected emergence**: The consolidation math. Realizing it would take 55 days of 100% network capacity to undo the shattering makes its permanence concrete. This isn't just dispersal—it's dispersal at a scale that can't be reversed.
