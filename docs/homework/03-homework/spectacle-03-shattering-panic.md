## Reflection

**What worked**: The countdown structure creates dread through accumulation. Each media clip adds to the sense of helpless momentum. The "economic waterboarding" line crystallizes the horror—it's not the damage, it's the rhythm.

**Emotional tone achieved**: Mounting hysteria with undercurrents of awe. Some voices are terrified, some are worshipful, some are analytical—but all are fixated. The shattering has become a global obsession.

**Serves the larger project by**: Showing the spectacle in action. The shattering isn't just a technical event—it's a media phenomenon, a psychological operation, a collective trauma. The Debordian analysis is implicit: we can't stop watching even as watching makes it worse.

**Technical integration**:
- The fee question is real and interesting (addressed in TikTok clip)
- The wallet counts follow actual exponential math
- The "rhythm as weapon" concept is psychologically grounded

**Genre elements**:
- Countdown clock as horror device
- Press secretary unable to answer
- The crowd watching together (streaming numbers)
- Expert analysis that explains without reassuring

**The rhythm IS the message**: Whether or not the emergence is "communicating," the six-hour intervals communicate inevitability, patience, and power beyond human intervention.
