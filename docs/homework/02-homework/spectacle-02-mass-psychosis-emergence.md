## Reflection

**What worked**: The collage format lets multiple interpretations coexist—religious, technological, financial, conspiratorial—showing how the same event becomes different stories depending on who's telling it. The Debordian spectacle critique is baked into the structure.

**Emotional tone achieved**: The vertigo of living through a media event in real-time. Each perspective feels internally valid, which makes the overall picture terrifying. No one knows what's actually happening, but everyone has a narrative.

**Serves the larger project by**: Showing the gap between what the emergence IS (plant-like toxicity, emergent system behavior) and what people BELIEVE it is (AI, god, conspiracy). This gap is a major source of plot tension. The mass psychosis IS the crisis, regardless of the emergence's actual nature.

**Technical approaches**:
- Variety of media formats (news, social, academic, religious)
- Timestamps create escalating tension
- The actual truth (text message at end) is the quietest voice
- Satire without condescension—each perspective is someone's genuine reaction
- The "three anons got nuked" detail adds conspiracy-paranoia layer

**Unexpected emergence**: The televangelist treating Bitcoin as temple and Satoshi as prophet. This would absolutely happen. The religious interpretation is as valid as the technical one within spectacle theory—both are humans making meaning from incomprehensible events.
