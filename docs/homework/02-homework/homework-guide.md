## Key Discoveries

### Elias Character Development
- Parkour as anxiety mitigation (trained without realizing)
- Physical competence that feels earned
- Pattern recognition as emotional regulation

### The Breaking Mechanic
- Proof-of-work authentication (skill can't be faked)
- Cultural insularity defeats infiltration
- Physical chaos defeats electronic surveillance
- Natural fit for chase/escape sequences

### Emergence Nature
- More plant-developing-toxicity than omnipotent-god
- Mass psychosis: what people BELIEVE vs what it IS
- The gap between reality and narrative drives plot tension

### Ending Options
- "Lost keys" provides anticlimax-as-profundity
- No faction wins because winning was never possible
- True to decentralization philosophy
