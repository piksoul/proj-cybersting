# Homework Phase 1 - Consolidated Questions

This document consolidates all questions raised during the first iteration of creative homework exercises. Each skill test generated questions that reveal gaps, opportunities, and decision points for the project.

---

## Questions by Category

### THE SABOTEUR (Central Mystery)

From **conceptual-diagram** and **speculative-insert**:
1. What does the Saboteur want? (Options: Idealist preventing weaponization, Thief redirecting wealth, Accelerationist triggering chaos, Emergent entity, Original Satoshi protecting creation)
2. Did the Saboteur know about the government's possession, or did they modify keys preemptively in 2011?
3. Is there a fourth layer of knowledge? (Someone who knows about the Saboteur?)
4. Is the "Saboteur" one person, a network, or possibly a non-human phenomenon?

From **speculative-insert** (Emergence Theory):
5. What if no human modified the keys—what if the modification emerged from the system itself?
6. Is "emergence" too abstract to sustain thriller tension?
7. What does the emergence "want"? Or is that the wrong question?
8. Who noticed the emergence before Elias? If anyone understood earlier, what did they do?
9. Does Satoshi know? Did they disappear because of it?
10. Is this the Spectacle achieving consciousness—Debord's theory made literal?
11. Should the emergence be revealed or left ambiguous?

From **puzzle-mystery-mechanic**:
12. How did the Saboteur have access to modify all three layers simultaneously?
13. Why 2011 specifically? What was happening that made it the right moment?
14. Why hasn't the Saboteur acted in 14 years? What are they waiting for?
15. Did the 2011 auditor really die in an accident? What did they see?

18. How does his paranoid pattern recognition differ from genuine paranoia?
19. What does he hide from himself about his situation?

## Priority Questions (Must Answer Before Drafting)

### Tier 1: Cannot Proceed Without Answers
1. **Who/what is the Saboteur?** (Human antagonist, network, emergence, or hybrid?)
2. **What happens at activation?** (Specific consequences must be known)
3. **What does Elias ultimately do?** (His choice = the climax)
4. **Where is the original signature chain?** (Plot mechanism needed)

### Tier 2: Should Answer for Coherent Draft
5. Chen's arc—is he redeemable, compromised, or tragic bureaucrat?
6. The 2011 auditor—what did they see, who killed them, why?
7. Elias's personal life—grounding details needed
8. Breaking scene integration—how does Berlin underground connect to main plot?

### Tier 3: Can Discover During Drafting
9. Specific Berlin locations and surveillance geography
10. Secondary character voices and dynamics
11. Spectacle media format details
12. Technical cryptography specifics (can be refined)

## Opportunities Identified

### From the Questions
- **The emergence theory** could elevate the novel beyond standard thriller into speculative territory
- **Chen's ambiguity** creates potential for complex institutional critique
- **The 2011 auditor subplot** provides classic thriller investigation mechanics
- **Berlin as surveillance landscape** offers atmospheric richness
- **Keys/locks motif** threads through every level of the narrative
- **The Spectacle broadcast** format could be powerful interstitial device

### Potential Combinations
- Chen as someone who noticed emergence years ago but buried it → explains his behavior
- The breaking scene as the only surveillance blind spot → why Elias goes underground
- Original signature chain held by Satoshi loyalists → introduces crypto-idealist faction
- The 2011 auditor's death connected to emergence-awareness → pattern of silencing
