## Craft Notes

**Form**: Concrete poetry / ASCII visualization sequence

**Technique borrowed from**:
- *Raw Shark Texts*: Visual typography as narrative content
- *House of Leaves*: Page as experience, not just carrier of text
- Flip-book aesthetics (the progression implies animation)

**Four visual movements**:
1. **The tree**: Classic binary split visualization, showing exponential branching
2. **The money**: Same concept but in dollar terms—abstract wealth becoming granular
3. **The scale contrast**: Single block vs. field of pixels—visual overwhelm
4. **The statement**: Text-as-image, the irreversibility made typographic

**Placement strategy**:
- Between chapters during the shattering week
- One page per visual (four separate interludes)
- Or combined as a single multi-page sequence at crisis peak

**Production consideration**: These work best as full pages with significant white space. The visual impact depends on isolation—don't crowd with other text.

**Thematic function**:
- The tree shows process
- The money shows stakes
- The scale shows incomprehensibility
- The statement shows meaning

**Length**: Each visual is ~1 page; together ~4 pages

**The Raw Shark parallel**: Hall's shark made of text attacks the protagonist. The shattering made of symbols attacks the reader's comprehension. Pattern as predator.
