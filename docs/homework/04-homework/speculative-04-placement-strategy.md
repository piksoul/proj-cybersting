# Interlude Placement Strategy

## Core Principles

### 1. Interludes as Rhythm Control

Interludes function like rests in music—they create shape through absence of main narrative. Too few, and the thriller becomes exhausting. Too many, and momentum dies.

**Target ratio**: One interlude every 3-4 chapters, with clustering during crisis sequences.

### 2. Interludes as Tonal Shift

Each interlude should provide contrast with surrounding material:
- After action → contemplative interlude
- After dialogue → visual/abstract interlude
- After conspiracy → historical interlude (epic distance)
- After personal → institutional interlude (cold perspective)

### 3. Interludes as Thematic Reinforcement

Interludes don't advance plot but deepen theme. They're where the novel's ideas can breathe without being dressed as dialogue or action.

## Interlude Type Map

| Type | Form | Best Placement | Function |
|------|------|----------------|----------|
| Historical Vignette | Reykjavik 1972, Genesis Block | Act breaks, section openings | Epic distance, pattern echo |
| Found Document | Redacted intel, academic paper | Before revelations | Create paranoia, official voice |
| Future Epigraph | Post-crisis historians | Chapter openings | Foreshadow, ironic hindsight |
| Pattern Prose | Flow state, network sensation | After action sequences | Palette cleanser, altered register |
| Visual/ASCII | Shattering cascade | Crisis peaks | Overwhelm, non-verbal meaning |
| Parallel Text | Debord commentary | Media/spectacle scenes | Philosophical grounding |

## Proposed Placement by Act

### ACT I: SETUP (Chapters 1-8)

**Chapter 1**: EPIGRAPH (academic, establishes hindsight frame)
> "The Shattering, as it came to be known..."

**Chapter 3 end**: HISTORICAL VIGNETTE (Satoshi's Silence)
- Establishes the fourteen-year silence before breaking it
- Creates reverence for what will be disrupted

**Chapter 6 end**: FOUND DOCUMENT (early classified memo)
- Hints at government key storage
- Bureaucratic voice contrasts with Elias's personal discovery

### ACT II-A: CONSPIRACY REVEALED (Chapters 9-16)

**Chapter 9**: EPIGRAPH (personal testimony)
> "I was twenty-three years old and I had seventeen Bitcoin..."

**Chapter 11**: PARALLEL TEXT (first Debord sequence)
- During first media coverage of anomaly
- Establishes spectacle theme

**Chapter 13 end**: HISTORICAL VIGNETTE (Reykjavik 1972)
- After chess-analyst character introduced
- Connects cold war thinking to present crisis

**Chapter 15**: VISUAL INTERLUDE (the tree—shattering begins)
- Pure visual as plot escalates
- No words needed

### ACT II-B: THE SHATTERING (Chapters 17-24)

**Chapters 17, 19, 21, 23**: EPIGRAPHS (rotating voices)
- Academic → Personal → Institutional → Philosophical
- Creates rhythm through consistency

**Chapter 18 end**: PATTERN PROSE (flow state)
- After Rotterdam chase sequence
- Elias's perception bleeding into narrative voice

**Chapter 20**: VISUAL SEQUENCE (full cascade)
- Mid-shattering, maximum visual overwhelm
- Can be 2-4 pages

**Chapter 22**: FOUND DOCUMENT (intercepted communication)
- The "hello" fragments
- Escalates mystery mid-crisis

**Chapter 24 end**: NETWORK SENSATION
- Before human climax, alien perspective
- The calm beneath the storm

### ACT III: RESOLUTION (Chapters 25-32)

**Chapter 25**: EPIGRAPH (the lost keys hint)
> "In the end, the keys were not stolen, seized, or destroyed..."

**Chapter 27**: PARALLEL TEXT (final Debord sequence)
- During climactic media saturation
- Theory meets practice

**Chapter 30 end**: HISTORICAL VIGNETTE (Genesis Block)
- Near the end, looking back to the beginning
- Circular structure

**Final page**: EPIGRAPH or SILENCE
- Either future historian summary
- Or: no interlude—just the story ending

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

1. **Back-to-back interludes**: Never two interludes without narrative between
2. **Interlude during action climax**: Don't interrupt chase/fight with meditation
3. **Explaining the interlude**: Never have characters discuss the interlude content directly after
4. **Repetitive form**: Vary interlude types—don't use three vignettes in a row
5. **Interlude as exposition dump**: Interludes illuminate theme, not plot mechanics

---

## Production Notes

**Typography considerations**:
- Interludes should have distinct visual treatment (font, spacing, headers)
- The horizontal dividers (───────) signal "this is different"
- Visual interludes need full-page treatment

**Length targets**:
- Epigraphs: 50-150 words
- Vignettes: 500-800 words
- Documents: 300-500 words
- Pattern prose: 400-600 words
- Visual: Variable (1-4 pages)

**Total interlude content**: Approximately 8,000-12,000 words in a 100,000-word novel (8-12%)

---

## Flexibility

This is a starting map, not a mandate. Actual placement will depend on:
- How chapters actually end up structured
- Where the writing naturally calls for breath
- Beta reader feedback on pacing
- Whether certain interludes earn more/less space

The strategy provides scaffolding. The novel will tell you where it needs to breathe.
