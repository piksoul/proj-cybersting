# Development Homework Tasks - Round 04

Focus on **structural interludes**—non-narrative elements that create rhythm, tone, and thematic depth without advancing plot.

## Research Summary

Six models studied for interlude techniques:

| Work | Technique | Key Lesson |
|------|-----------|------------|
| *World War Z* | Oral history fragments | Multiple voices, global scope, documentary illusion |
| *House of Leaves* | Footnotes, appendices | Layers of text, gaps as meaning, reality blurring |
| *Raw Shark Texts* | Visual typography | Text as image, page as experience, flip-book |
| *Watchmen* | Comic-within-comic | Parallel commentary, thematic echo, structural rhyme |
| *Dune* | Future epigraphs | Epic distance, foreshadowing, unreliable framing |
| *Station Eleven* | Object as connection | Artifact linking characters, mirror for action |

## Interlude Types Developed

### Historical Vignettes
- **Reykjavik 1972**: Fischer-Spassky, cold war chess, pattern for contemporary crisis
- **Satoshi's Silence**: The fourteen-year quiet before it breaks
- **Genesis Block**: Origin moment, circular structure for ending

### Found Documents
- **SIGINT Intercept**: Redacted intelligence report with "hello" fragments
- Creates institutional voice, paranoia through gaps

### Future Epigraphs
- Collection of 10+ potential chapter openers
- Academic, personal, institutional, philosophical voices
- Creates epic distance, foreshadowing, tragic irony

### Abstract/Pattern
- **Flow State Poem**: Parkour perception as prose poetry
- **Network Sensation**: Speculative phenomenology of emergence
- Non-narrative, sensory-dominant, altered register

### Visual
- **Shattering Cascade**: ASCII visualization sequence
- Four movements: tree, money, scale, statement

### Parallel Text
- **Debord Commentary**: Society of the Spectacle as counterpoint
- Overlaid or adjacent structure for media/spectacle scenes

## Placement Strategy Summary

```
ACT I:        E——V——D————————
ACT II-A:     E——P——V—————————
ACT II-B:     E—E—E—E—P—V—V—D—N  (maximum density during crisis)
ACT III:      E——P——V——————————

E=Epigraph V=Vignette D=Document P=Prose/Parallel N=Network
```

Target: ~8-12% of total word count in interludes (~8,000-12,000 words in 100K novel)

## Key Craft Principles

1. **Interludes as rhythm**: Like rests in music—shape through absence
2. **Contrast with surroundings**: After action → contemplative; after dialogue → visual
3. **Theme not plot**: Interludes deepen meaning, don't advance story
4. **Visual distinction**: Typography signals different register
5. **Flexibility**: The map is scaffolding; the novel will show where it needs breath
