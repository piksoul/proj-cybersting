# Location Reference

Concise location data for prose-agent scene/sensory slot population. Each entry: Name, City/Region, Narrative function, Sensory palette, Scene notes.

## Act I Locations

### Elias's Training Space — Berlin
- **Function**: Crew home base. Where Elias is himself. Opening scenes.
- **Sensory palette**: Light-industrial unit. Concrete floor worn smooth in distinct landing zones from years of practice (evidence compressed into the floor). Marked zone taped for the cipher. High ceilings — seven metres — lost in shadow past the reach of yellow work lights on tripod stands (Dominik's arrangement). Exposed brick buried under graffiti layered geological, oldest tags bled into the mortar. Portable speaker at the edge pushing bass. Water bottles scattered everywhere. Layers of clothing stacked against walls. Sweat on skin cooling fast — warm inside from bodies, winter outside.
- **Scene notes**: Intimate, physical, grounded. Contrasts with every institutional space later. Sound of bodies landing, breath, shoes on concrete. **NO chalk dust** — breakers don't chalk (palms need to slide). See `ref-culture-breaking.md` for subcultural rules and greeting hierarchy.

### Elias's Apartment — Berlin
- **Function**: Private space. Where the call comes. Domestic tension.
- **Sensory palette**: Sparse, functional. Kitchen counter with a dark phone face-down. Careful ordinariness — nothing personal on the walls. TBD further detail.
- **Scene notes**: Reveal character through absence. What's missing matters more than what's there.

### Berlin Streets (Night)
- **Function**: Transitional. Movement, surveillance awareness, urban texture.
- **Sensory palette**: Sodium lights pooling on wet pavement. Turkish corner shops spilling fluorescent glow. Graffiti layered so deep the oldest tags are geological. Distant club bass through walls. Security cameras on every third building. Tram rattle. Wet cobblestones. Bicycle bells from nowhere.
- **Scene notes**: Surveillance is weather here — ambient, constant. Elias notices cameras without meaning to.

### The SCIF — United States
- **Function**: Institutional power. Key ceremony failure. Inciting crisis.
- **Sensory palette**: Subterranean, beneath Treasury/NSA. Concrete walls, no windows. Blue-white LED strips along ceiling channels. HSM racks humming behind glass. Cold recycled air with a metallic taste. Pressure doors that sigh when they seal. The sound of protocol failing.
- **Scene notes**: Ritual space. The ceremony is liturgical — and it breaks. Tension from procedure collapsing, not from action.

## Act II Locations

### Berlin (Extended)
- **Function**: Deepening home terrain. Hacker culture, history of surveillance.
- **Sensory palette**: Ex-Stasi data vaults repurposed as server rooms. Underground clubs (Tresor-like) — concrete, strobe, sweat. Street-level political graffiti arguing with itself. Hacker-space soldering irons and cold coffee.
- **Scene notes**: Berlin knows what surveillance states look like. The city remembers.

### Prague
- **Function**: Early contacts. Paranoia builds. Gothic unease.
- **Sensory palette**: Gothic spires against grey sky. Kafkaesque alleys narrowing to nothing. Vltava river fog erasing the bridges. Hacker-hostels with tangled cables. Absinthe bars lit green.
- **Scene notes**: Everything feels watched. Architecture as paranoia made physical.

### Budapest — Szechenyi Bathhouse
- **Function**: Clandestine meeting. "Where surveillance fails."
- **Sensory palette**: Neo-Baroque columns rising from steam. Outdoor pools, sulphur smell, skin flushed red. Old men playing chess on floating boards. Fog so thick cameras are blind. Voices distorted by water and echo.
- **Scene notes**: The one place no one can record. Intimacy forced by architecture. Bodies exposed, secrets exchanged.

### Zurich
- **Function**: The raid. Multiple factions converge. Crypto-fortress breached.
- **Sensory palette**: Clean streets, private vault, precision architecture. Security that looks like politeness. The moment the vault opens — loss of control.
- **Scene notes**: Order collapsing. Swiss precision meeting global chaos.

### Singapore
- **Function**: Financial hub. High-rise espionage. Algorithmic scale.
- **Sensory palette**: Glass towers, trading floors humming with data. Stringent security — biometric everything. Heat and humidity outside, refrigerated air inside. Algorithmic trading dens lit by scrolling numbers.
- **Scene notes**: Money as infrastructure. The system made visible.

### Split/Dubrovnik — Croatia
- **Function**: Safehouse under pressure. "Old world vs new chaos."
- **Sensory palette**: Medieval stone walls warm to the touch. Narrow alleys forcing single file. Adriatic light — hard, white, no shadows to hide in. Salt air. Ancient fortifications repurposed.
- **Scene notes**: Nowhere to hide in a city built for hiding. History as irony.

### Istanbul — Modern Art Museum
- **Function**: East-West junction. Encrypted handoff.
- **Sensory palette**: Bosphorus skyline through floor-to-ceiling glass. Contemporary installations — data sculpture containing the message. Guards who look at art. Ferries sounding horns outside.
- **Scene notes**: The message is in the art. Hiding information in plain sight at the literal crossroads.
