## Crisis Clock

- Crisis peaks in **December**. The natural deadline: markets, governments, and emergence all converging on the same window.
- Story world is set **~2035** (approximately 10 years from 2025).
- The ticking clock is felt, not explained. Characters reference "before the new year" — the audience feels the countdown.
