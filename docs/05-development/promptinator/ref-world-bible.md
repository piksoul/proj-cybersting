# World Bible — Prose Agent Reference

Compressed reference for scene-slot generation. Not a planning doc. Consult per scene.

---

## Era & Setting

Near-future, roughly ten years ahead. Never date precisely. Close enough for plausibility, far enough for speculative tech. The world is recognisable but slightly worse.

**Geography:** Global story. U.S. for institutional power. Europe/Berlin for chase sequences and subculture. International flashpoints as needed.

**Visual defaults:**

- **Cities at night:** neon, sodium, LED wash, cold air.
- **Institutional spaces:** sterile, fluorescent, humming. Power expressed as boredom.
- **Subculture spaces:** warm, loud, human. Resistance expressed as presence.
- **Natural world:** appears rarely and pointedly. When it shows up, it means something.

Surveillance is ambient, not dystopian. Corporate infrastructure is fortress-like. Street level clings to analogue.

---

## Narrative Voice (Locked)

- Third person limited, past tense.
- POV distance varies by scene type:
  - **Remote** — scene-setting, establishing shots.
  - **Near** — action, procedural sequences.
  - **Close** — emotional confrontation, dialogue-heavy.
  - **Intimate** — internal revelation, turning points.
- **Elias POV:** 85-95% of scenes. Reliable on facts, unreliable about himself. Minimises feelings. Limited self-knowledge. Walls as architecture.
- **Architect POV:** 5-15%. Optional. Used sparingly for counterpoint.
- Voice: technical precision, controlled emotion.
- **No head-hopping mid-scene.** POV shifts only at chapter or section breaks.

## Technology

Rule: if a reader Googles it, they should find a real-world analogue. Everything feels like today but slightly better.

**Cryptographic / Bitcoin:**

- **HSMs** (Hardware Security Modules) — physical devices for key operations. Central to key ceremonies.
- **Multi-signature / Shamir Secret Sharing** — keys split into shards. Real Bitcoin capability.
- **Key ceremonies** — formal, witnessed procedures for activating or rotating keys. Highly ritualised. Treat as ritual, not lecture.
- **Quantum-resistant signing modules** — background texture only.
- **Rogue nodes / governance handshakes** — unauthorised coordination on the Bitcoin network.
- **AI-assisted forensic blockchain analysis** — state-level tool. Elias has direct experience.
- **"Prometheus"** — codename for the key management system Elias helped build.

**Surveillance & Counter-surveillance:**

- **Ambient surveillance** — sensors, cameras, biometric scanners everywhere. Not totalitarian. Like weather.
- **Drone curtains** — automated formations scanning biometrics at borders, airports, perimeters.
- **Spoofed biometrics / facial-morph camouflage** — countermeasures exist. Expensive. Unreliable.
- **Digital shadows** — persistent data trails. Escaping yours is a plot-level achievement.

**Communications:**

- **Darknet comms** — eerie, minimal UIs. Functional, not stylish. Terminal green.
- **Cached AI agents** — communicating in sub-symbolic languages. Background texture only.
- **Off-grid communication** — difficult. Physical proximity, dead drops, legacy infrastructure. Going off-grid is a story event, not a convenience.

## Geopolitical Backdrop

- U.S. secretly holds ~1.1M BTC (Satoshi wallets). Plans **"New Bretton Woods"** — dump gold, crash EM reserves, reveal BTC holdings, transition to a Bitcoin-backed reserve hybrid.
- BRICS accumulating gold for a post-dollar world. The U.S. BTC reveal would devastate this strategy.
- EU fracturing under pressure. Middle Eastern funds hedging into BTC. IMF/BIS in quiet panic.
- **Inciting incident:** U.S. attempts to activate the Satoshi keys. Signature fails. Sabotage confirmed. Keys were swapped years earlier — a 2011 modification by the Architect.
- **The Triple Theft:** someone steals the keys, then the emergence steals them from the thieves.

## Thematic World Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **No side is fully good.** Moral ambiguity is default.
2. **Technology is texture, not exposition.** Key ceremony = ritual, not lecture.
3. **Surveillance is weather.** Characters live with it, adapt, occasionally escape.
4. **Money is a story.** Currencies are collective fictions enforced by power.
5. **The network is deeper than anyone's understanding.** Mystery preserved.
6. **Code is a character.** Not literally — but it has presence, weight, consequence.
