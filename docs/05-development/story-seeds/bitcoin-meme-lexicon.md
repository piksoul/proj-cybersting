# Bitcoin Meme Lexicon

A collection of Bitcoin/crypto community phrases, memes, and cultural shorthand. These can be:
- Used directly by characters (especially maximalists, traders, or online chatter)
- Adapted/subverted for ironic effect
- Placed in background media, social feeds, or Spectacle Inserts
- "Cookietized" — twisted into something darker or more resonant

---

## Core Maxims

| Phrase | Meaning | Story Potential |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **"Not your keys, not your coins"** | If you don't control the private keys, you don't own the Bitcoin | Central to our premise — the Satoshi keys question |
| **"Not your keys, not your cheese"** | Playful variant; same message, rodent energy | Lighter touch; comic relief character; meme-literate dialogue |
| **"Don't trust, verify"** | Trustless systems; check the blockchain yourself | Elias's journey from trust to verification |
| **"Few understand"** | Dismissive flex; implies speaker has rare insight | Useful for arrogant characters; ironic when wrong |
| **"Have fun staying poor"** | Smug dismissal of skeptics | Dated cringe energy; good for satire |
| **"We're still early"** | Adoption is nascent; opportunity remains | Can be hopeful or delusional depending on speaker |
| **"1 BTC = 1 BTC"** | Bitcoin's value is intrinsic; ignore fiat price | Philosophical core — what IS value? |
| **"Bitcoin fixes this"** | Applied to any societal problem | Maximalist absurdity; satire potential |
| **"Tick tock, next block"** | Network keeps producing blocks regardless of drama | Emergence theme — the system doesn't care |

## Emotional States / Reactions

| Phrase | Meaning | Story Potential |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **"HODL"** | Hold on for dear life (originated from typo) | Classic; character under pressure refusing to sell/act |
| **"Diamond hands"** | Holding through volatility without selling | Resolve; stubbornness; possibly foolish |
| **"Paper hands"** | Selling at first sign of trouble | Weakness; pragmatism; knowing when to fold |
| **"Rekt"** | Wrecked — lost everything | Aftermath; character who bet wrong |
| **"WAGMI"** | We're all gonna make it | False hope; community copium; sometimes sincere |
| **"NGMI"** | Not gonna make it | Dismissal; dark humor; resignation |
| **"Rug pull"** | Scam where developers abandon project with funds | Betrayal; institutional version possible |

## Dialogue Fragments

Ready-to-use snippets for character voice:

**Maximalist true believer:**
> "Stack sats, ignore the noise, verify don't trust. The protocol doesn't care about your feelings. Tick tock, next block."

**Cynical trader:**
> "HODL is just cope for people who bought the top. Smart money has paper hands and a plan."

**Disillusioned early adopter:**
> "We used to say 'few understand.' Turns out we were the few who didn't."

**Analyst explaining to outsider:**
> "They say 'not your keys, not your coins.' What they don't tell you is that your keys can become someone else's keys. Then what?"

**Dark humor in crisis:**
> "WAGMI? Look around. NGMI is the optimistic take at this point."

## Usage Notes

- **Background chatter:** Social media feeds, forum posts, talking-head media can use these raw
- **Character voice:** Maximalists, traders, crypto-native characters should use these fluently
- **Outsider perspective:** Non-crypto characters hearing these should find them cult-like or absurd
- **Subversion:** The cookietized versions work when reality breaks the meme's assumptions
- **Spectacle Inserts:** Rapid-fire meme collages can establish cultural texture
