## RACHEL

### Background

Colleague at Treasury/NSA. The affair happened during Elias's high-pressure years. It was real — not just stress or opportunity. They both felt something.

Then David found out. Rachel was transferred. Elias was investigated. The relationship was terminated along with his career trajectory.

**Now:** Unknown. Transferred to another posting. May still be in the system. May have left entirely.

### Psychology

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Toward Elias | Unknown — anger? Grief? Still feeling something? |
| Her cost | Career damaged, marriage destroyed, moved away |
| Possible states | Remarried and moved on? Bitter? Still carrying it? |

### Voice

> "Don't. Just... don't. You don't get to show up now and explain."

> "I lost everything too, Elias. You weren't the only one who paid."

> "I thought about reaching out. A hundred times. But what would I say?"

## DAVID

### Background

Rachel's husband. Project manager in the same sphere — how they all knew each other. When he discovered the affair, he didn't just end the marriage. He systematically destroyed Elias's career.

**Now:** Still in the system. Still vindictive. Has never forgotten.

### Psychology

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Motivation | Revenge (dressed as justice) |
| Method | Bureaucratic warfare, investigations, blocked promotions |
| Toward Elias | Pure hatred, justified in his mind |
| Blind spot | His revenge has become his identity |

### Voice

> "You think I forgot? I haven't forgotten. I won't ever forget."

> "Everything that happened to you after — that was me. Every door that closed. Every opportunity that vanished. Me."

> "She's moved on. I haven't. Some of us have principles."
