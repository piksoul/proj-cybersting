## Who She Was

### Personality

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Three adjectives** | Smart, fun, fast |
| **Her gift** | Saw PEOPLE where Elias saw PATTERNS |
| **Her flaw** | Too trusting of the system. Too brave. Too loyal to wrong people. |
| **Irony** | Her speed/precision was her undoing — she was fast enough to be on that mission |

### What She Represented

- **Hope** — Believed in the system
- **Conscience** — Honest player in dishonest world
- **Humanity** — Saw people, not data points
- **Competence** — Better at physical work than Elias (took his place on mission)

### The Spark

> She's the spark leading Elias to breaking/parkour scene — her physical competence inspires his later journey.

## Relationship to Elias

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **How they met** | Work colleagues (same agency/department) |
| **Nature of friendship** | Close platonic friends. Initial tension → professional respect → real friendship |
| **Dynamic** | She was always matchmaking for him. Saw him clearly. |
| **Key contrast** | She saw PEOPLE; he saw PATTERNS. Neither alone was enough. |

### The Double Irony

- Elias sees patterns but couldn't save her
- She couldn't see patterns but saw HIM clearly
- His analytical gift failed when it mattered most
- Her human instinct might have saved her if she'd seen the betrayal coming

## Her Death

### The Mission

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Official description** | Maintenance / physically moving infrastructure |
| **Reality** | Crossed paths with Network operations |
| **How she died** | Killed by crossfire OR killed in cleanup to keep things quiet |
| **Connection to main plot** | Tangential — her death opened door to Elias's discovery, not directly responsible |

### What Elias Did

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **How he found out** | Decrypted communication he wasn't supposed to see |
| **What he tried** | Warn Sarah directly + alert superior |
| **Why it failed** | Too late (mission in progress), wasn't believed, actively blocked |
| **Aftermath** | Crushing guilt, burnout, isolation |

### What He Doesn't Know

- He only knows she died — not exactly how
- Official story conflicts with what he suspects
- **Narrative power**: The uncertainty haunts him more than facts would
- His imagination fills the gaps (worse than reality?)

### What He Suspects

> The operation wasn't about an external asset. It was about an internal threat. Someone on the project was selling information. The "operation" was a hit disguised as a security action. Sarah wasn't a target — she was in the wrong place. Or she was the target, and everything he thought he knew about her was wrong.

## Thematic Function

Sarah represents what Elias lost AND what he's fighting for:

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Her gift** (seeing people) | What Elias needs to learn |
| **Her death** | Cost of isolation / failing to act with others |
| **Her memory** | Motivation to not let it happen again |
| **Her echo** | The voice that calls him back to humanity |

**The lesson**: Pattern-sight alone couldn't save her. People-sight alone couldn't save her. You need BOTH. The crew gives Elias both — and he must accept it.
