## Background

German intelligence liaison assigned to keep tabs on American assets in Berlin. Elias is one of several people Weber monitors. Monthly check-ins, filed reports, maintained distance.

Weber knows Elias is burned out. Weber prefers it that way — a quiet asset is a good asset.
