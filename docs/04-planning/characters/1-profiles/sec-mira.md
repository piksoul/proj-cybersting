## Background

Mira and Elias were involved during his Prometheus years. She was the one person who knew both versions of him — the brilliant analyst and the compromised, unraveling man.

She left. Not dramatically — just quietly withdrew when she saw what he was becoming. The relationship ended not with betrayal but with her recognizing he couldn't be saved by love.

**What happened**: She tried to reach him. He pushed her away. She let herself be pushed. Clean break, but with weight.
