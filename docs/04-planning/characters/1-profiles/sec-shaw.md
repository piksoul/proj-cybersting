## Background

### Career History

Former NSA analyst who saw too much, asked too many questions, and got reassigned to career Siberia.

> Still has contacts. Still has access. Deeply bitter.

### What Happened

- Worked within the system (NSA/intelligence)
- Saw something that disturbed them
- Asked the wrong questions
- Got pushed out — not fired, just made irrelevant
- Now operates in the grey zone between factions

### Relationship to Elias

> They knew each other slightly during Prometheus. Shaw reached out after Elias's exile. They've never met in person.

- Not close during active service
- Shaw made contact after Elias left
- Digital relationship only — encrypted channels
- Shaw sees Elias as someone who might understand
- Elias sees Shaw as potentially useful, potentially compromised

## Arc

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Arc Type** | Growth or Fall — TBD |
| **Starting State** | Trapped, bitter, watching |
| **Possible Endings** | Freed or destroyed |
| **Function** | Shows whether escape from systems is possible |

### Arc Possibilities

**Option A: Redemption Arc (Growth)**
- Shaw helps Elias despite risk
- Acts selflessly when it matters
- Achieves the freedom they sought
- *Proves escape IS possible*

**Option B: Sacrifice Arc (Fall that becomes Growth)**
- Shaw helps Elias but pays the price
- Dies ensuring Elias succeeds
- Achieves freedom only through death
- *Heroic sacrifice — common in genre*

**Option C: Betrayal Arc (Fall)**
- Shaw was playing both sides all along
- Betrays Elias at critical moment
- Was never really free, never could be
- *Proves some can't escape the system*

**Option D: Ambiguous**
- Shaw helps, then disappears
- Unclear if they survived or were captured
- Escape remains uncertain
- *Preserves mystery*

If Shaw dies, it should demonstrate:

## Genre Considerations

### The Defector Archetype in Spy Fiction

Defectors in the genre typically:
- Provide crucial information
- Die before the end (often sacrificially)
- Sometimes turn out to be double agents
- Rarely escape cleanly

**Subverting expectation**: Shaw survives and genuinely escapes
**Meeting expectation**: Shaw dies heroically or is revealed as compromised
**Complicating expectation**: Shaw's motives remain ambiguous to the end
