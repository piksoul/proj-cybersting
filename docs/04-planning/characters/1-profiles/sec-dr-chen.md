## Background

The reason Elias entered the field. Legendary cryptographer, Elias's mentor at NSA/Treasury. Praised Elias, guided him, seemed like a father figure.

Then the catastrophe: Chen's last commit caused the security failure. An operative died. Elias took the blame. Chen never reached out, never defended him, never explained.

**Still active.** Still respected. Still dangerous.
