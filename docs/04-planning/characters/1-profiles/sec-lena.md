## Background

Alliance operative assigned to honey trap Elias. Unlike typical operations, something changed. Whether she developed feelings or just grew disgusted with her handlers, she went rogue.

Instead of delivering Elias's pillow talk confessions to the Alliance, she leaked them to journalists and crypto anarchists. Then she disappeared.

**Now:** Running, hiding, surviving. Both Alliance and whoever she embarrassed want her dead.
