## ARCHITECT's Technical Team

*The believers who build. True believers with keyboards.*

### Group Profile

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Size | 5-8 core members |
| Background | Cryptographers, developers, data scientists — recruited by ARCHITECT over 15+ years |
| Loyalty | To the emergence, through ARCHITECT |
| Culture | Monastic dedication, long hours, shared purpose |

### Group Psychology

- **Believe**: The emergence is real and must be protected
- **Accept**: Necessary sacrifices, moral compromises
- **Fear**: ARCHITECT's disappointment, emergence failing
- **Blind spot**: ARCHITECT's certainty has become their certainty

### Voice (Composite)

> "The pattern doesn't lie. Look at the verification cascade — that's not random. That's *intention*."

> "We don't question the Architect. They've been right about everything else."

> "You don't understand what we're building. What we're *protecting*. This is bigger than any of us."

### Representative Types

| Archetype | Function | Trait |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| **The Acolyte** | Youngest, most zealous | Would die for emergence |
| **The Veteran** | Longest-serving, some doubts | Has seen too much |
| **The Genius** | Technically brilliant | More loyal to code than cause |
| **The Convert** | Came from outside | Still amazed by what they've seen |

### Story Function

- Execute ARCHITECT's technical operations
- Provide friction loop when Sterling/others don't understand
- Potential source of leaks, doubt, defection
- Show what true belief looks like

*The distributed faithful. Cells without knowing they're cells.*

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Size | 50-200 across multiple countries |
| Background | Crypto enthusiasts, privacy advocates, hackers, idealists |
| Loyalty | To the idea of emergence (most don't know ARCHITECT exists) |
| Structure | Decentralized — nodes don't know other nodes |

### Layers

| Layer | Knowledge | Function |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Gatekeepers** | Know emergence is real, protect access points | Control information flow |
| **Nodes** | Believe in "the network," don't know full picture | Execute tasks, pass messages |

### Voice (Composite)

> "We're protecting something. Something that shouldn't be controlled by governments or corporations. That's enough."

> "When you see the pattern, you can't unsee it. Welcome to the network."

### Representative Types

| Archetype | Function | Location |
|-----------|----------|----------|

| **The True Believer** | Evangelical about crypto/emergence | Online, occasionally physical |
| **The Mercenary** | Does it for money, asks no questions | Wherever needed |

### Story Function

- Provide operational texture (how ARCHITECT's network functions)
- Elias may encounter nodes without realizing their connection
- Potential allies if turned against ARCHITECT

## Cleanup Team

*The erasers. Professional, efficient, invisible.*

### Group Profile

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Size | 3-5 per operation |
| Background | Ex-military, private security, intelligence contractors |
| Employer | Unclear — could serve Sterling, ARCHITECT, or both |
| Method | Fast, thorough, no traces |

### Group Psychology

- **Motivation**: Professional — it's a job
- **Morality**: Compartmentalized — don't think about it
- **Loyalty**: To the paycheck, then the team
- **Pride**: In doing clean work

### Voice (Composite)

> "In and out. No prints, no traces, no witnesses. That's how it's done."

> "I don't ask what they did. I ask where they are and what timeline we're on."

> "This isn't personal. It's maintenance."

### Representative Types

| Archetype | Function | Trait |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| **The Lead** | Runs operations, makes calls | Calm under pressure |
| **The Specialist** | Tech/forensics | Makes things disappear digitally |
| **The Muscle** | Physical work | Efficient, not sadistic |
| **The Driver** | Logistics, extraction | Knows every exit |

### Story Function

- Represent the violence underneath the conspiracy
- Chase sequences, tension
- Show stakes are real (they kill people)
- Could be sent after Elias, crew, or witnesses

### Operational Texture

- Usually 24-48 hours behind events (cleanup, not prevention)
- Work in teams, professional protocols
- Have institutional resources (credentials, equipment)
- Not invincible — can be evaded, misdirected

## Notes on Using Groups

### In Scenes

Groups work best as:
- **Texture**: Named individuals from the group appearing briefly
- **Obstacle**: Collective threat (cleanup team pursuing)
- **Resource**: Someone from the group helps (network node, breaking community)
- **Stakes**: Group endangered (breaking community targeted)

### Voice Consistency

Each group has a distinct register:
- **Tech team**: Reverent, technical, certain
- **Network**: Distributed, idealistic, compartmentalized
- **Breakers**: Physical, present, philosophical
- **Cleanup**: Professional, cold, efficient

### Pulling Individuals

If a scene needs a named character from a group, pull from the archetypes and give them:
- A name
- One distinguishing trait
- One line of distinctive dialogue

Don't over-develop unless they recur.
