## Thematic Function

Martinez embodies **Control** in its institutional form:

| Elias | ARCHITECT | Martinez |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Learning to release control | Wants total control | Control through procedure |
| Adapts | Obsesses | Manages |
| Individual | Prophet | Bureaucrat |

She's not the extreme (that's ARCHITECT) — she's the mundane face of control. The forms to fill out. The protocols to follow. The system that grinds on regardless of individuals.

### Mirror Function

Martinez shows what Elias could have become if he'd stayed:
- Professional, capable, respected
- Part of the machine
- Complicit in everything the machine does
- Unable to see beyond institutional logic
