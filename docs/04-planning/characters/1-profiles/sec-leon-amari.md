## Background

### Origin

Born in Marseille to Algerian immigrant parents. Grew up in the northern districts — not the postcard version of the city. Learned early that the body was a tool for survival, expression, and escape.

### Movement History

| Period | Development |
|--------|-------------|
| **Teens** | Capoeira and street dance |
| **20s** | Competitive b-boy — regional champion, never quite international |
| **Late 20s** | Moved to Berlin, following the scene and escaping something in France |
| **Since then** | 20 years building reputation as teacher, mentor, community anchor |

### The Murky Past

Leon doesn't discuss his years between leaving Marseille and arriving in Berlin. Possibilities:
- Foreign Legion (would explain physical discipline, certain scars)
- Time in North Africa with family/other work
- Simply hard years he'd rather forget
- Nothing dramatic — just private

> **Story note:** His past doesn't need to be revealed unless it serves the plot. The mystery is part of his character. He doesn't ask about others' pasts because he doesn't want to discuss his own.

## Relationship to Elias

### How They Met

Elias started showing up at the outdoor sessions about two years into his Berlin exile. Kept to himself, trained hard, asked no questions. Leon recognized the type — trained somewhere, running from something, using movement to manage it. He gave Elias space.

Over months, respect developed. Leon occasionally offered corrections, observations. Elias listened without ego. They never had a "getting to know you" conversation. They just became part of each other's lives.

### Current Dynamic

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Leon's role** | Mentor without claiming the title; gives space, occasional wisdom |
| **Elias's role** | Student who doesn't need teaching; fellow traveler |
| **Unspoken understanding** | Both carry weight; neither asks about it |
| **Trust level** | Leon would help Elias without asking why; Elias knows this |

### What Leon Sees in Elias

> A man running from something he can't outrun. Smart enough to know it. Using movement to manage what he can't solve. There's something coiled in him — violence or grief or both. Leon recognizes it because he's seen it in the mirror.

### What Elias Sees in Leon

> Proof that it's possible. A man who carries his own weight but found a way to live with it. The calm Elias can't achieve. A mentor who teaches by example, not instruction.

### What Leon Gives Elias

1. **Flow state practice** — Quiets pattern-sight
2. **Acceptance** — Doesn't judge his past
3. **Rhythm** — Alternative to control
4. **Permission** — To stop carrying alone
5. **Model** — Of what peace looks like

## Arc

### The Foresight

- Feels the circle turning
- Knows his time is ending (not morbidly — peacefully)
- Sensed something in Elias from the beginning
- Has been preparing for his own exit

### The Disappearance (Both Variations)

- After climax, Leon simply... isn't there
- Maybe a note, maybe nothing
- Not death — choice
- He was never going to stay
- He'd been walking toward an ending his whole life
- This time, he gets to choose it

**Why disappearance, not death**:
- Avoids Obi-Wan trap (mentor sacrifice as manipulation)
- His exit is CHOICE, not sacrifice
- Shows wisdom, not martyrdom
- Leaves open-ended (could return if needed)
- More Buddhist than Christian

### His Exit Beat

> "He was never going to stay. He'd been walking toward an ending his whole life. This time, he got to choose it."

**Not death. Disappearance. Freedom.**
