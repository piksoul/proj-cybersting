## Character Profiles

### KESSLER — The Whale

#### Background

Hedge fund founder who was early to crypto:
- Net worth in billions
- Has funded both legitimate research and illegal operations
- Pragmatic about methods, focused on returns
- Treats people as assets to acquire

#### Psychology

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Core trait** | Acquisitive — everything is a potential holding |
| **Strength** | Unlimited resources, patience, strategic thinking |
| **Weakness** | Can't understand things that aren't transactional |
| **What he wants** | Edge — information, people, systems that give advantage |

#### Relationship to Elias

> Wants to hire him. If that fails, wants to own him. If that fails, wants to remove him as a variable.

- Sees Elias as a valuable asset
- Doesn't care about ideology
- Pure resource calculus

#### Voice

Casual about billions, intense about edges:

> "I don't need you, Elias. I want you. There's a difference. The first is negotiation. The second is... acquisition."

> "Everyone has a price. The interesting question is never 'will they sell?' — it's 'what currency do they accept?'"

> "I've bought governments. You think I can't buy you?"

#### Story Function

| Function | How |
|----------|-----|
| **Market perspective** | Shows capital's view of the crisis |
| **Financial temptation** | Offers Elias money/safety |
| **Resource disparity** | Demonstrates what Elias is up against |
| **Threat escalation** | Final offer/threat in Act 3 |

### SANTOS — The Fixer

#### Background

Former investment banker turned crypto fixer:
- Knows everyone
- Facilitates everything
- Judges nothing
- Has arranged deals between cartels and governments, cults and corporations
- Everything is a transaction

#### Psychology

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Core trait** | Amoral neutrality — services all sides |
| **Strength** | Universal access, no allegiances |
| **Weakness** | No principles mean no protection when things get personal |
| **What he wants** | Continued relevance — to be needed by everyone |

#### Relationship to Elias

> Potentially useful to both sides. Willing to facilitate anything for the right fee. Has information Elias needs — at a price.

- Neutral party
- Information broker
- Could help OR hurt Elias depending on who pays

#### Voice

Smooth, utterly transactional:

> "I can get you what you need. I can also get your enemies what they need. No judgment. Just market rates."

> "I don't have clients. I have counterparties. The distinction matters."

> "Loyalty is a premium service. You want it, you pay for it. Otherwise, I'm just a facilitator."

#### Story Function

| Function | How |
|----------|-----|
| **Information broker** | Has what Elias needs — at a price |
| **Neutral party** | Services all factions |
| **Market amorality** | Everything is transaction |
| **Cross-faction character** | Exists at faction intersections |

**Kessler as sole Market face**:
- Financial power + threat
- Direct antagonist presence
- Acquisitive energy drives scenes

**Santos's broker function handled by**:
- Dominik (Berlin underground)
- Other faction contacts
- Anonymous market intermediaries

### Option B: Keep Santos, Cut Kessler

**Santos as sole Market face**:
- Broker/fixer energy
- Neutral party creates more complex scenes
- Information as leverage

**Kessler's wealth function handled by**:
- Reference to "market players"
- PMC contractors as physical manifestation
- Financial pressure without named face

### Option C: Combine Into One Character

**KESSLER-SANTOS hybrid**:
- Wealthy fixer who operates above the market

- Can offer AND threaten
- One character serves both functions

## Thematic Function

Market operators represent **Capital's indifference**:

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Amorality** | Money has no ideology |
| **Resource disparity** | Elias can't outspend them |
| **Transaction** | Everything is buyable (they think) |
| **Limitation** | Can't buy what matters (connection, trust, meaning) |

### Contrast with Other Factions

| Faction | What They Want | How They Pursue |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Directorate** | Control | Authority, threat |
| **Alliance** | Leverage | Diplomacy, temptation |
| **Zealots** | Belief | Ideology, manipulation |
| **Market** | Profit | Money, acquisition |

Market is the most honest: they just want to own things. No ideology pretends otherwise.
