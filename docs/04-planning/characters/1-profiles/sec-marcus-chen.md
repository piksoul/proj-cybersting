## Background

Charismatic founder who built a crypto project that turned out to be a massive fraud. At its peak, it had billions in market cap. When it collapsed, people lost everything. Some killed themselves.

Elias met him at the Austin conference. Had a recorded conversation that makes Elias look complicit. Whether Marcus drew him in deliberately or Elias just had bad timing is unclear.

**Now:** Unknown. Possibly:
- Dead (suicide, murder, faked)
- Imprisoned (cooperating? Silent?)
- Disappeared (new identity, still has money)
- Resurfaced (running new scheme)
