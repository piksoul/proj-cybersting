## Background

### Profile

Chinese diplomatic/intelligence hybrid:
- Educated at Oxford
- Speaks perfect English
- Genuinely charming
- Absolutely ruthless when necessary
- Has studied Elias's work, knows his papers

> The civilised face of Chinese intelligence. Not a thug — an intellectual who happens to work for a state that will do whatever is necessary.

### The Hybrid Role

Chen Wei exists at the intersection of diplomacy and intelligence:
- Official diplomatic cover
- Actual intelligence operative
- Can engage on intellectual level
- Can pivot to threat when needed
- Represents the "soft" approach vs Alliance muscle

## Psychology

### Core Traits

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Charming** | Genuinely likeable in person |
| **Intellectual** | Engages with ideas, respects competence |
| **Patient** | Long-term thinking, not rushed |
| **Steel underneath** | Ruthlessness when required |
| **Admiring** | Genuinely respects Elias's abilities |

### What He Wants

> He wants Elias to "come in from the cold" to a better employer. The Alliance values talent. They would "maintain their investments more carefully" than the Americans.

### His Offer

The Alliance alternative:
- Appreciation of Elias's skills
- Protection from Directorate
- Resources to continue his work
- A new home, new purpose
- The implicit threat: or else

---

## Voice

Warm, intellectual, occasional steel:

> "The Americans built you, used you, and threw you away. We would... maintain our investments more carefully."

> "I'm not here to threaten you, Elias. I'm here to offer you a future. The distinction matters."

> "You see patterns others miss. We value that. We don't punish it."

> "You think you're running from them. But you're running toward something. Let me show you what."

### Voice Pattern

- Cultured English (Oxford-educated)
- Warm opening, steel when needed
- Never crude threats — implications
- Intellectual respect as approach
- Long sentences, patient rhythm

### What Chen Wei Sees in Elias

> A wasted asset. Someone the Americans built, used, and discarded. Someone who could be valuable to the right employer. Someone who might be convinced — or compelled.

### What Elias Sees in Chen Wei

> A tempting alternative wrapped in a civilised package. The offer is real. So is the danger. Chen Wei represents a different kind of cage — gilded but still a cage.
