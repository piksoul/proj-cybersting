## Background

### Origin

Born in Berlin in 1993, three years after reunification. Grew up in a city still finding itself — East and West bleeding together, old systems collapsing, new ones emerging. His parents were East Germans who adapted quickly; they taught him that connections matter more than ideology.

### Education of a Fixer

Dominik learned early:
- Everyone needs something
- Most things can be found if you know who to ask
- Discretion is worth more than the transaction
- Never be the only one holding a secret

By his early twenties, he was the person people called when they needed things — club entry, party supplies, documents, introductions. Nothing violent, nothing ideological. Just the grease that keeps underground Berlin moving.

### The Movement Connection

Came to breaking through the club scene — same circles, overlapping communities. He's not competition-level, but he trains enough to belong. The movement scene is his social life; the fixing is his business. He keeps them separate except when someone needs something.

## Relationship to Elias

### How They Met

Through the movement scene, two years ago. Dominik noticed the American who trained seriously but stayed quiet. Recognized something — not a cop, not a user, just a guy with a past he wasn't discussing. Dominik respects that. He has his own things he doesn't discuss.

### Current Dynamic

They're friendly but not friends. Dominik knows Elias could be a useful contact — clearly has skills, clearly has history, clearly has money from somewhere. Elias knows Dominik could be useful if things go sideways. Neither has tested the relationship.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Dominik's view** | Elias is a potential client who hasn't bought anything yet |
| **Elias's view** | Dominik is a resource to be used carefully |
| **The boundary** | Neither has asked the other for anything real. Yet. |

### Trust Level

> Dominik wouldn't betray Elias casually — bad for reputation, and he genuinely likes him. But if serious pressure came (legal, financial, physical), Dominik would prioritize himself. Elias probably knows this. It's why he hasn't asked for anything important.

### The Conflict

- Minor but real during buildup
- Dominik's disappointment when Elias doesn't commit fully
- "You're thinking too much. Again."
- Too picky, too demanding
- But this is what Elias needs — someone who won't accept less
