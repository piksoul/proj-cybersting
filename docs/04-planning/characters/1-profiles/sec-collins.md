## Background

Collins managed the Prometheus project — not the technical work, but the logistics, timelines, personnel. Made the trains run on time. Didn't care about ethics or brilliance, just deliverables and compliance.

When Elias fell, Collins signed the reassignment paperwork. Didn't fight for him. Didn't fight against him. Just processed it.
