# Elias Alternative Characterisations

## Overview

Each backstory creates a fundamentally different person. This document maps how Elias's core traits, social style, voice, and emotional defaults shift based on kompromat choice.

---

## Core Elias (Constant Across All)

```
BASELINE ELIAS
├── Brilliant pattern recognition
├── Cryptographic/systems expertise
├── Built Prometheus key management
├── Estranged from father
├── In Berlin, practicing breaking/parkour
├── Burned out, withdrawn
└── Christmas promise pending
```

**What changes:** WHY he's burned out, HOW he relates to people, WHAT he fears about himself.

---

## Character Variations

### #1: Party Drugged (Gibson)

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Escapism—uses substances to quiet his mind |
| **Social style** | Functional addict, performs normalcy |
| **Self-image** | "I'm in control" (but isn't) |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition is exhausting, needs chemical dimming |
| **Trust pattern** | Trusts himself least of all |
| **Emotional default** | Controlled numbness punctuated by chaos |

**This Elias:** The high-functioning mess. Appears together, crumbling inside. His brilliance comes with a cost he's been self-medicating.

**Voice:** Precise, clipped, occasionally too sharp. Humor used as deflection. Sentences that start strong and trail off. Irritable when questioned about personal habits.

**Physical tells:** Checks pockets frequently (where he keeps pills). Pupils slightly off. Micro-tremor when he hasn't taken anything.

### #3: Confession (Robin Hood)

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Need to be understood—will open up to the wrong person |
| **Social style** | Guarded but desperately wants connection |
| **Self-image** | "I can read people" (but couldn't read her) |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition failed when it mattered most |
| **Trust pattern** | Craves intimacy, terrified of it |
| **Emotional default** | Loneliness masked as independence |

**This Elias:** The lonely genius. Brilliant at systems, terrible at people (or so he believes). His confession wasn't stupidity—it was human need.

**Voice:** Thoughtful, occasionally over-explains, long pauses before personal revelations. Asks questions that seem professional but are really personal. Changes subject when conversation gets too intimate.

**Physical tells:** Leans in when someone shares something personal. Catches himself, pulls back. Holds eye contact too long, then breaks it completely.

### #4a: Aviation Short

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Moral flexibility under pressure—did wrong thing for wrong reasons |
| **Social style** | Avoids financial/wealth discussions |
| **Self-image** | "I'm not that person anymore" (but is he?) |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition as weapon he used for profit |
| **Trust pattern** | Doesn't trust himself in systems with incentives |
| **Emotional default** | Quiet self-loathing, penance through withdrawal |

**This Elias:** The reformed predator. Was a shark, now vegetarian. But the teeth are still there.

**Voice:** Careful with money talk, deflects discussions of profit. Occasional flashes of old ruthlessness when cornered—sharp, predatory observations that surprise even him. Apologizes after.

**Physical tells:** Flinches at financial news. Walks past expensive things without looking. Lives below his means conspicuously.

### #4b: Crypto Scam

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Carelessness with words—showing off has consequences |
| **Social style** | Now excessively careful about what he shares |
| **Self-image** | "My mouth kills people" |
| **Relationship to gift** | Knowledge is dangerous when shared |
| **Trust pattern** | Doesn't trust himself to explain things safely |
| **Emotional default** | Guilt, overcaution, verbal minimalism |

**This Elias:** The silenced oracle. Used to love explaining things, teaching. Now every word is weighed for damage potential.

**Voice:** Terse. Answers questions with questions. Visibly uncomfortable when asked to elaborate. Occasionally slips into old enthusiastic explanation—leaning forward, speaking faster, hands moving—then catches himself. Goes quiet. Sometimes stops mid-sentence.

**Physical tells:** Hand moves to mouth when he's said too much. Writes things down instead of saying them. Deletes messages before sending.

### #6: Chen Betrayal

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Hero worship—gave trust to someone who didn't deserve it |
| **Social style** | Hypervigilant about mentors, authority figures |
| **Self-image** | "I can't tell friend from enemy" |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition works on systems, not people |
| **Trust pattern** | Deep skepticism of anyone who seems helpful |
| **Emotional default** | Paranoia, analysis paralysis on relationships |

**This Elias:** The burned disciple. Once believed in mentorship, now sees every teacher as potential Judas.

**Voice:** Probing questions, never takes statements at face value. Visible discomfort with praise or guidance. "Why are you helping me?" as genuine question. Deflects compliments about his work.

**Physical tells:** Positions himself with back to wall. Notes exits. Watches hands. Doesn't accept food or drink he didn't see prepared.

### #7a: Fight Club Burnout

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Nihilism—sees manipulation everywhere, meaning nowhere |
| **Social style** | Detached observer of human theater |
| **Self-image** | "I see too clearly to believe in anything" |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition revealed the void |
| **Trust pattern** | Trusts no system, no institution, maybe no person |
| **Emotional default** | Flat affect, occasional sardonic observation |

**This Elias:** The burned-out prophet. Saw behind all the curtains, found nothing worth believing in.

**Voice:** Dry, observational, occasionally profound, mostly exhausted. Dark humor. Quotes things ironically. "Does it matter?" as genuine question, not rhetorical. Perks up only for genuine human moments, then remembers to be cynical.

**Physical tells:** Slouches. Doesn't bother with appearance. Eyes track patterns in crowds, billboards, screens—then look away in disgust. Only fully present during physical practice.

### #7b: Can't Shut Up

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Compulsive truth-telling—can't let wrongness stand |
| **Social style** | Blurts corrections, challenges authority |
| **Self-image** | "Someone has to say it" (but learned no one wants to hear) |
| **Relationship to gift** | Sees problems clearly, can't stop pointing them out |
| **Trust pattern** | Expects to be silenced, monitors for signs |
| **Emotional default** | Frustrated righteousness, suppressed anger |

**This Elias:** The muzzled whistleblower. Born to speak truth to power, trained into silence through pain.

**Voice:** Measured... until it isn't. Visible effort to hold back. Bites tongue literally. When he finally speaks freely, it's a torrent—everything he's been holding. Immediately regrets it. "Forget I said that."

**Physical tells:** Jaw tight. Shoulders high. Hands grip things. Releases explosively then goes still. Checks over shoulder after saying anything substantive.

### #7c: Affair

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Boundary failure—let personal and professional merge |
| **Social style** | Now rigidly compartmentalized |
| **Self-image** | "I let my guard down and lost everything" |
| **Relationship to gift** | Irrelevant—this was a human failure |
| **Trust pattern** | Avoids workplace intimacy completely |
| **Emotional default** | Walled off, bristles at closeness |

**This Elias:** The compartmentalizer. Once let things bleed together. Never again.

**Voice:** Professional, boundaried, noticeably uncomfortable with personal questions. "That's not relevant." Changes subject from anything non-operational. Softens only with clear non-work contacts.

**Physical tells:** Maintains physical distance. Doesn't touch. Stiffens if touched. Clear workspace, no personal items. Leaves social events early.

### #8+9: Bad Week Combined

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Self-doubt—can't trust his own perception OR his own physicality |
| **Social style** | Second-guesses constantly, checks impressions |
| **Self-image** | "I might be seeing things that aren't there. I might hurt someone." |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition might be genius or madness |
| **Trust pattern** | Doesn't trust himself, seeks external validation |
| **Emotional default** | Anxious, hyperaware, physically careful |

**This Elias:** The uncertain weapon. Doesn't know if his greatest asset is real or delusion, if his body is tool or threat.

**Voice:** Qualifies statements ("I think," "it seems like," "unless I'm wrong," "correct me if—"). Asks for confirmation. Physically contained, controlled movements. Apologizes preemptively. "Does that make sense? Am I making sense?"

**Physical tells:** Keeps hands visible, movements deliberate. Avoids crowds. Startles at contact. Practices precise physical movements (parkour) as proof he can control his body.

### #10: Kill Switch + Sarah

| Trait | This Elias |
|-------|------------|
| **Core flaw** | Cassandra complex—being right doesn't save anyone |
| **Social style** | Bitter about institutions, warnings, process |
| **Self-image** | "I tried to tell them. No one listened. People died." |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition showed him the future; it didn't matter |
| **Trust pattern** | Doesn't trust systems to respond correctly to truth |
| **Emotional default** | Grief-tinged anger, suppressed "I told you so" |

**This Elias:** The ignored prophet. Saw the disaster coming, was dismissed, now lives in the aftermath.

**Voice:** Clipped when discussing institutions. Softens around genuine human connection. Occasional flash of old warmth when reminded of Sarah. Bitter one-liners about process, hierarchy, chains of command. "Let me guess—they need a committee."

**Physical tells:** Tenses during institutional interactions. Relaxes with individuals. Carries something of Sarah's (photo? object?). Touches it unconsciously.

## Recommended Combo Character

**#10 (Kill Switch + Sarah) + #4b (Crypto Scam):**

| Trait | Combined Elias |
|-------|---------------|
| **Core flaw** | Words and warnings both fail—carelessness kills, but so does being ignored |
| **Social style** | Terse but occasionally bitter; careful with knowledge, cynical about process |
| **Self-image** | "I've hurt people by talking too much AND by being right. What's left?" |
| **Relationship to gift** | Pattern recognition is both weapon and curse—dangerous to share, useless to keep |
| **Trust pattern** | Doesn't trust himself to share safely OR institutions to listen |
| **Emotional default** | Guarded grief layered over older guilt |

**Voice:**
- Terse—measures words
- Qualifies when sharing knowledge ("this could be wrong")
- Bitter about institutional failure ("they won't listen anyway")
- Softens unexpectedly around genuine human moments
- Occasional flash of old enthusiasm quickly suppressed
- Sometimes starts explaining something complex, catches himself, goes silent

**Physical tells:**
- Hand to mouth when he's said too much
- Tenses during institutional interactions
- Carries something of Sarah's
- Practices parkour as the one place he trusts his body and mind together

**This Elias is:**
- A man who's been wrong (crypto) AND right (kill switch), and people died both times
- Neither optimistic about sharing nor about warning
- Caught between silence and speech, both of which have cost lives
- Deeply human underneath the armor—Sarah's death proves he CAN connect

**His arc:** Learning that the problem wasn't speaking or staying silent—it was acting alone. He needs allies, collaboration, people who will actually listen AND act. The emergence forces him to find that.
