# Character Arc Mapping

## Purpose

Understanding how characters change (or don't) across the narrative. Maps internal transformation to plot structure.

Reference: `docs/craft/character-arcs.md`

---

## Arc Types Reference

| Arc Type | Direction | Character at End |
|----------|-----------|------------------|
| **Positive Change** | Flaw → Strength | Transformed for the better |
| **Growth** | Limited → Wiser | Improved version of self |
| **Disillusionment** | Optimist → Realist | Lost innocence, gained truth |
| **Fall** | Good → Corrupted | Moral decline |
| **Corruption** | Flawed → Destroyed | Flaw consumes them |
| **Flat** | Consistent | Changes the world, not self |

## Elias Reed - Protagonist Arc

### Arc Type Options

| Option | Arc Type | Starting State | Ending State | Fit with Story |
|--------|----------|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| **A** | Positive Change | Isolated, controlling, trust-averse | Connected, accepting, trusting | Classic hero journey; Christmas promise fulfilled |
| **B** | Growth | Skilled but wounded | Skilled and healed | Less dramatic change; refinement |
| **C** | Disillusionment | Believes systems can be controlled/understood | Learns emergence transcends understanding | Bittersweet; loses certainty, gains truth |
| **D** | Fall | Good intentions | Becomes what he fights | Dark ending; tragedy |

### Working Hypothesis: Disillusionment with Positive Elements

Elias undergoes **disillusionment** about control/systems but **positive change** regarding connection/trust.

- He loses his belief that mastery = control
- He gains the ability to trust, connect, rely on others
- Bittersweet: the emergence is beyond him, but that's okay because he's not alone

### The 7-Stage Arc Process for Elias

| Stage | Description | Our Story |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| 1. **Initial State** | Flaws, issues, weaknesses, skewed worldview | *Isolated, self-reliant, haunted by kompromat, believes control = safety* |
| 2. **Catalyst** | Something challenges beliefs, highlights flaws | *Triple Theft pulls him back in; past catches up* |
| 3. **Resistance & Struggle** | Internal and external obstacles | *Tries to solve it alone; old methods; factions close in* |
| 4. **Self-Reflection & Learning** | New insights, recognition | *Found family shows another way; ARCHITECT as dark mirror* |
| 5. **Action & Transformation** | Taking action to change | *Chooses to trust; asks for help; reveals vulnerability* |
| 6. **Climax & Resolution** | Ultimate test of growth | *Final confrontation; must rely on others; flow state communion* |
| 7. **New State** | New strength, some flaws overcome | *Connected, at peace with uncertainty, Christmas promise kept* |

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] What specific moment marks his transformation?
- [ ] Does he fully overcome his flaw or just begin to?
- [ ] How does the arc connect to the thematic question?

## ARCHITECT - Antagonist Arc

### Arc Type Options

| Option | Arc Type | Trajectory | Implications |
|--------|----------|------------|--------------|
| **A** | Flat (Dark) | Was always this way; changes the world | Pure antagonist; ideology never wavers |
| **B** | Corruption | Brilliant visionary → megalomaniac absorbed | Shows how good intentions corrupt |
| **C** | Fall | Started with genuine insight → lost humanity | Tragic antagonist; could have been Elias |
| **D** | Revealed Flat | Appears to change but was always the same | Twist reveals true nature |

### Working Hypothesis: Corruption Arc (Already Complete)

ARCHITECT's corruption arc happened **before the story** - we see the endpoint.

- Was once a genuine visionary (Satoshi-adjacent?)
- Glimpsed emergence and became obsessed
- Lost humanity pursuing transcendence
- By story start, corruption is complete

This allows:
- Backstory revelation about who he was
- Tragic dimension (what he sacrificed)
- Contrast with Elias (who faces similar temptation)
- His "arc" in the story is actually **flat** - testing Elias

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] Do we show ARCHITECT's backstory or leave mysterious?
- [ ] Is there any moment of doubt or humanity in him?
- [ ] What was his original wound that led to corruption?
- [ ] How does his fate embody thematic consequences?

## Father - Supporting Character Arc

### Arc Type Options

| Option | Arc Type | Relationship to Elias Arc |
|--------|----------|--------------------------|
| **A** | Growth | Father also learns/grows; mutual reconciliation |
| **B** | Flat | Father remains consistent; Elias must change to reconnect |
| **C** | Catalyst only | No arc; exists to catalyse Elias's change |

### Working Hypothesis: Flat Arc (Catalyst)

Father has a **flat arc** - he represents the truth Elias needs to learn.

- He's always valued connection, family, presence
- He's been waiting for Elias to come home
- His consistency is what Elias needs
- The Christmas promise represents the theme

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] Does Father have any development, or is he a fixed point?
- [ ] What is Father's understanding of Elias's past?
- [ ] Does Father play active role in climax or is resolution post-climax?
