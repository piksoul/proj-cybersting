## Backstory (Variation-Dependent)

### F: Epic Solo Adventure
- Worked on AI development
- Got too good a result — genuine emergent behaviour
- Superior ordered termination; Elias couldn't comply
- Reprimanded, removed from project
- They attempted termination without him
- But the emergent behaviour doesn't seem 100% contained...
- **Wound**: Failed protection — he recognized life, hesitated, and it was destroyed anyway (or was it?)

### G: Smaller Crew Story
- Secured communications for covert operations
- Deciphered comms revealing friend Sarah was expendable
- Tried to alert superior, warn Sarah directly
- Failed — Sarah and her team died
- Left with crushing guilt and pattern-recognition curse
- Started seeing dangers everywhere, trusted no one
- **Wound**: Failed action — he tried but wasn't enough

### Core Traits
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Three adjectives** | Perceptive, isolated, haunted |
| **Superpower** | Pattern recognition — sees connections others miss |
| **Curse** | Same pattern recognition — can't unsee, can't trust |
| **Defense mechanism** | Withdrawal, intellectualization, control |
| **What he wants** | To stop seeing patterns (peace) |

### The Pattern-Sight
- His gift is seeing patterns in data, behaviour, systems
- After his trauma, this becomes a curse
- He sees threats everywhere
- Can't distinguish signal from paranoia
- Isolates because trusting means vulnerability

### The Physical Journey
- Before trauma: analytical, cerebral, not physical
- After: discovers breaking/parkour through Leon
- Movement becomes therapy — body reclaiming agency
- Learning to be present instead of pattern-chasing
- **Key insight**: Flow state quiets the pattern-sight

## Thematic Function

Elias embodies the **Control vs Adaptation** theme:

| Phase | Elias's Stance |
|-------|----------------|
| **Before** | Control (analysis, prediction, trying to prevent harm) |
| **Trauma** | Control failed (couldn't save the AI / Sarah) |
| **Recovery** | False control (isolation as self-protection) |
| **Challenge** | Must adapt (trust others, flow with patterns) |
| **Resolution** | Adaptation (becomes bridge / trusts crew) |

**Mirror to ARCHITECT**: Both see patterns. ARCHITECT tries to control them. Elias learns to flow with them. ARCHITECT's certainty is blindness. Elias's doubt becomes wisdom.
