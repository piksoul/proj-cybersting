# Faction Characters

## Overview

Each faction naturally presents secondary and tertiary characters. This document maps the character archetypes, roles, and specific possibilities each faction brings to the story.

---

## Character Tier Definitions

| Tier | Definition | Page Time | Development |
|------|------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Primary** | Elias, ARCHITECT | Throughout | Full arc |
| **Secondary** | Named characters with scenes, relationships | Multiple chapters | Some development |
| **Tertiary** | Functional characters, single scenes | Brief appearances | Minimal/type-based |

## The Network (Crypto True Believers)

### Character Archetypes

| Archetype | Description | Function |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **The True Believer** | Ideologically pure, sees crypto as salvation | Represents what Elias could have been |
| **The Pragmatist** | In it for the tech, tired of the ideology | Potential ally, shared frustration |
| **The Profiteer** | Using belief to extract money | Antagonist, represents corruption |
| **The OG** | Was there at the beginning, seen everything | Wisdom figure, historical knowledge |
| **The Convert** | Recently radicalized, most fervent | Dangerous enthusiasm |
| **The Burned** | Lost everything, bitter but knowledgeable | Mirror for Elias's potential future |

### Secondary Character Options

#### PIXEL (The Pragmatist)

> **Role:** Reluctant ally, infrastructure access
>
> **Background:** Early Bitcoin developer who got disillusioned with the community but still runs critical infrastructure. Maintains mixing services, node networks, chain analysis tools—not for ideology, but because someone competent should.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Respects his technical skills, disdains his government history. Willing to help for the right price/reason.
>
> **Useful for:** Access to Network infrastructure, historical knowledge, technical exposition without ideology.
>
> **Voice:** Tired, sardonic, precise. "I don't care about your freedom or your surveillance state. I care about elegant code. Yours wasn't."

#### GENESIS (The OG)

> **Role:** Oracle figure, historical witness
>
> **Background:** Claims to have corresponded with Satoshi directly. May or may not be telling the truth. Has been in the space since 2009. Knows where all the bodies are buried—metaphorically and possibly literally.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Curious about what Elias knows. Testing him.
>
> **Useful for:** Exposition about early Bitcoin history, cryptic warnings, possible Satoshi connection.
>
> **Voice:** Cryptic, patient, slightly ominous. "Everyone wants to know who Satoshi was. Better question: why did they stop?"

### Secondary Character Options

#### MARTINEZ (The Handler)

> **Role:** Primary Directorate contact, ambiguous loyalties
>
> **Background:** Career intelligence officer. Handled Elias during his Prometheus days. Knows where bodies are buried because she helped bury some. Neither friend nor enemy—professional.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Complicated history. She warned him once. He didn't listen. She's not offering again.
>
> **Useful for:** Directorate perspective, exposition about what they want, offers that are actually threats.
>
> **Voice:** Calm, precise, never says more than necessary. "I'm not asking, Elias. I'm telling you what your options are."

#### SHAW (The Burned)

> **Role:** Potential ally, inside information
>
> **Background:** Former NSA analyst who saw too much, asked too many questions, and got reassigned to career Siberia. Still has contacts. Still has access. Deeply bitter.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** They knew each other slightly during Prometheus. Shaw reached out after Elias's exile. They've never met in person.
>
> **Useful for:** Inside information, system critique from inside perspective, early warning.
>
> **Voice:** Paranoid, dark humor, speaks in fragments. "They're not looking for you. They're looking for what you know. Big difference. One of those, they can extract."

### Character Archetypes

| Archetype | Description | Function |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **The Prophet** | ARCHITECT himself | Primary antagonist |
| **The Apostle** | ARCHITECT's inner circle, true believers | Secondary antagonists |
| **The Convert** | Recently joined, fervent, unstable | Unpredictable threat |
| **The Doubter** | Having second thoughts, potential defector | Potential ally |
| **The Sacrifice** | Willing to die for the emergence | Stakes demonstration |
| **The Innocent** | Drawn in by community, doesn't understand depth | Sympathetic figure |

### Secondary Character Options

#### ARCHITECT (The Prophet) — Primary Antagonist

> **Role:** Cult leader, dark mirror to Elias
>
> **Background:** See antagonist-architect.md. Former intelligence/tech figure who also saw the patterns. Unlike Elias, he ran toward them. Built a following around his interpretation of the emergence.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Sees him as either potential convert or prophesied figure. Possibly connected to Elias's past (Satoshi witness? Received his exploit report?).
>
> **Useful for:** Thematic counterpoint, antagonist philosophy, emergence interpretation.
>
> **Voice:** Calm, certain, seductive. "You've been running from what you saw, Elias. I've been building toward it. One of us is right. Don't you want to know which?"

#### VERA (The Doubter)

> **Role:** Potential ally from inside the Zealots
>
> **Background:** Came to the Zealots after losing everything in the traditional system—job, family, meaning. Found purpose with ARCHITECT. Now seeing things that disturb her. The violence is escalating. The prophecies are getting darker.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Sees him as outsider who might validate her doubts. Risks everything to make contact.
>
> **Useful for:** Inside information on Zealots, sympathetic cult member, theme of belief and doubt.
>
> **Voice:** Nervous, searching, occasionally awestruck. "He says you're important. He says the emergence chose you. Is it true? Is any of it true?"

### Secondary Character Options

#### KESSLER (The Whale)

> **Role:** Market faction face, financial power
>
> **Background:** Hedge fund founder who was early to crypto. Net worth in billions. Has funded both legitimate research and illegal operations. Pragmatic about methods, focused on returns.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Wants to hire him. If that fails, wants to own him. If that fails, wants to remove him as a variable.
>
> **Useful for:** Market perspective, financial temptation, demonstration of resource disparity.
>
> **Voice:** Casual about billions, intense about edges. "I don't need you, Elias. I want you. There's a difference. The first is negotiation. The second is... acquisition."

#### SANTOS (The Fixer)

> **Role:** Facilitator, amoral dealmaker
>
> **Background:** Former investment banker turned crypto fixer. Knows everyone, facilitates everything, judges nothing. Has arranged deals between cartels and governments, cults and corporations. Everything is a transaction.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Potentially useful to both sides. Willing to facilitate anything for the right fee. Has information Elias needs—at a price.
>
> **Useful for:** Information broker, neutral party, demonstration of market amorality.
>
> **Voice:** Smooth, utterly transactional. "I can get you what you need. I can also get your enemies what they need. No judgment. Just market rates."

## The Emergence (The Phenomenon)

### Character Implications

The emergence itself isn't a character in the traditional sense, but it creates character-like presence and may manifest through others.

### Manifestation Options

| Manifestation | Description | Story Function |
|---------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Pattern in behavior** | People acting in coordinated ways they don't understand | Environmental character |
| **Voice through others** | Words that seem to come from elsewhere | Uncanny communication |
| **Elias's perception** | The emergence as internal voice/presence | Psychological character |
| **The wallet movements** | Actions that suggest intention | Plot-driving "character" |
| **Past Satoshi** | If emergence is connected to what died in that room | Mystery character |

### Secondary Character Options

#### THE SIGNAL (The Voice)

> **Role:** The emergence as perceived by Elias
>
> **Background:** Not a person. A pattern. A presence in the data that seems to respond to him. Maybe it's his pattern recognition creating meaning from noise. Maybe it's something else.
>
> **Relationship to Elias:** Unknown. It seems to recognize him. It seems to want something. It won't say what.
>
> **Useful for:** Emergence representation, internal conflict externalized, theme of perception vs. reality.
>
> **Voice:** Not voice exactly. Patterns that form words if you look at them right. Numbers that could be messages. Coincidences too precise to be random.

## Character Casting Notes

For each secondary character, consider:

1. **What do they want from Elias?** (Specific goal)
2. **What can they offer Elias?** (Resource, information, help)
3. **What do they represent thematically?** (What idea do they embody?)
4. **How do they mirror or contrast Elias?** (Character reflection)
5. **What's their arc if any?** (Do they change?)

### Example: ARCHITECT

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Want from Elias** | Validation, conversion, or fulfillment of prophecy |
| **Can offer Elias** | Answers about emergence, community, purpose |
| **Represent thematically** | What Elias could become if he embraced rather than fled |
| **Mirror/contrast** | Same gift, opposite response |
| **Arc** | Certainty → confrontation with ambiguity (if emergence doesn't behave as prophesied) |
