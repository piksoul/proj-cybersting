## Background

### Origin

Born in Marseille to Algerian immigrant parents. Grew up in the northern districts—not the postcard version of the city. Learned early that the body was a tool for survival, expression, and escape.

### Movement History

- Started with capoeira and street dance in his teens
- Competitive b-boy through his 20s—regional champion, never quite international
- Transitioned to teaching as his body aged out of competition
- Moved to Berlin in his late 20s, following the scene and escaping something in France
- Built a reputation over 20 years as teacher, mentor, community anchor

### The Murky Past

Leon doesn't discuss his years between leaving Marseille and arriving in Berlin. Possibilities:
- Foreign Legion (would explain physical discipline, certain scars)
- Time in North Africa with family/other work
- Simply hard years he'd rather forget
- Nothing dramatic—just private

**Story note:** His past doesn't need to be revealed unless it serves the plot. The mystery is part of his character. He doesn't ask about others' pasts because he doesn't want to discuss his own.

## Personality

### Core Traits

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Calm** | Nothing rattles him visibly; has seen enough |
| **Economical** | Uses few words; lets silence work |
| **Perceptive** | Notices everything, comments on little |
| **Patient** | Teaches through repetition, not explanation |
| **Protective** | Of his community, his students, his people |
| **Private** | Shares little of himself; respects others' privacy |

### Philosophy

Leon believes in:
- **Presence over past/future**: "Be here now, or fall"
- **The body as teacher**: Movement reveals what words hide
- **Community over competition**: The scene matters more than any individual
- **Quiet healing**: Some wounds need space, not talk
- **Earned trust**: Given slowly, not easily broken

### Flaws

- **Avoidant**: Won't address problems directly if he can wait them out
- **Closed**: His privacy can read as coldness
- **Passive**: May not intervene when he should
- **Haunted**: Whatever he left in France still visits him sometimes

## Relationship to Elias

### How They Met

Elias started showing up at the outdoor sessions about two years into his Berlin exile. Kept to himself, trained hard, asked no questions. Leon recognized the type—trained somewhere, running from something, using movement to manage it. He gave Elias space.

Over months, respect developed. Leon occasionally offered corrections, observations. Elias listened without ego. They never had a "getting to know you" conversation. They just became part of each other's lives.

### Current Dynamic

- **Leon's role**: Mentor without claiming the title; gives space, occasional wisdom
- **Elias's role**: Student who doesn't need teaching; fellow traveler
- **Unspoken understanding**: Both carry weight; neither asks about it
- **Trust level**: Leon would help Elias without asking why; Elias knows this

### What Leon Sees in Elias

> A man running from something he can't outrun. Smart enough to know it. Using movement to manage what he can't solve. There's something coiled in him—violence or grief or both. Leon recognizes it because he's seen it in the mirror.

### What Elias Sees in Leon

> Proof that it's possible. A man who carries his own weight but found a way to live with it. The calm Elias can't achieve. A mentor who teaches by example, not instruction.

## Story Function

### Thematic Role

Leon represents:
- **Present-moment awareness** (vs. Elias's pattern-obsession with past/future)
- **Healing through practice** (what Elias is attempting)
- **Chosen family** (vs. blood family estrangement)
- **Wisdom without answers** (he doesn't solve problems, he reframes them)

### Plot Functions

| Function | How |
|----------|-----|
| **Safe harbor** | Gym as location; his trust as resource |
| **Wisdom delivery** | Key lines that echo at climax |
| **Stakes** | Part of what Elias is protecting |
| **Witness** | Sees Elias's transformation, comments obliquely |

### Chekhov's Gun

If Leon is introduced with his philosophy about presence and flow, **that philosophy must matter at the climax**—when Elias needs to trust his perception and enter flow state to commune with the emergence.

## Key Scenes

### Act 1: Training Scene

> Leon watched Elias move through the sequence. Good form. Good control. Too much control.
>
> "You're fighting it," Leon said.
>
> Elias paused. "Fighting what?"
>
> "The fall. You're not supposed to prevent it. You're supposed to redirect it." Leon demonstrated—a drop that became a roll that became a rise. "You don't beat gravity. You dance with it."

### Act 2: Safe Harbor

> "I need somewhere to stay. Somewhere no one knows about."
>
> Leon studied him for a long moment. Whatever he saw, he didn't ask about.
>
> "The gym has a back room. No cameras. No questions." He handed Elias a key. "Don't bring trouble to my students."
>
> "And if trouble finds me anyway?"
>
> Leon's expression didn't change. "Then we deal with it. That's what family does."

### Act 2/3: Departure

> "You're leaving." Not a question.
>
> Elias didn't answer. He was packing what little he'd brought.
>
> "Whatever this is," Leon said, "whatever you're running toward now instead of away from—remember what I taught you."
>
> "The wall doesn't care about my problems?"
>
> Leon almost smiled. "The wall doesn't care. But you do. That's the difference between surviving and living."

## Notes for Development

### Questions to Resolve

- Does Leon's past become relevant, or remain mystery?
- Does he provide active help (safe house, connections) or just wisdom?
- Is he present at the end, or is his role complete by Act 3?
- Does he meet any faction characters, or stay in the "normal world" lane?

### Potential Expansion

If Leon needs more plot relevance:
- His past could connect to intelligence world (Legion contacts, etc.)
- He could have his own history with the emergence (dismissed it years ago)
- He could be threatened by factions to get to Elias
- He could provide a crucial resource at a key moment

### Keep in Mind

Leon works best as a **grounding presence**, not an action character. His value is wisdom, calm, and the proof that healing is possible. Don't over-complicate him unless the story demands it.
