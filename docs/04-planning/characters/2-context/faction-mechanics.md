# Faction Mechanics: Currencies, Coordination & Conflict

**Purpose**: Reference document for how factions actually operate - the currencies they trade, how they coordinate, how they respond to crisis, and where moral actors exist.

---

## Part 1: The Currency Stack

**Money is ONE currency, not THE currency.**

### Primary Currencies

| Currency | What It Is | Who Has It | How It Flows |
|----------|------------|------------|--------------|
| **Money/Credit** | Financial resources | Banks, states, criminals, tech | Transactions, laundering, grants |
| **Information** | Intelligence, secrets, data | Agencies, hackers, insiders | Trade, leaks, surveillance |
| **Leverage/Kompromat** | Ability to compel | Those who collect it | Threats, "understandings" |
| **Access** | Being in the room | Gatekeepers, networked individuals | Introductions, appointments |
| **Legitimacy** | Moral/legal cover | States, institutions, NGOs | Endorsements, partnerships |
| **Violence Capability** | Kinetic options | Military, criminal, mercenary | Contracts, deniable ops |
| **Narrative Control** | Shape what people believe | Media, platforms, influencers | Placement, spin, suppression |
| **Technical Capability** | What you can actually do | Engineers, hackers, scientists | Recruitment, contracts, theft |
| **Loyalty/Ideology** | True believers | Movements, cults, patriots | Indoctrination, shared cause |
| **Plausible Deniability** | Distance from action | Cutouts, fronts, proxies | Layers, compartmentalization |

### Intangible Currencies

| Currency | Description | Story Relevance |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Reputation** | What others believe about you | Elias has burned reputation; must rebuild |
| **Trust** | Rare and precious | Network operates on it; Elias has lost it |
| **Attention** | In attention economy, this is power | The Spectacle trades in this |
| **Time** | Some actors can wait, others can't | Emergence is patient; governments aren't |
| **Silence** | What you don't say | Elias's value is partly what he hasn't revealed |
| **Debt/Favors** | Obligations owed | "You owe me" is currency |
| **Fear** | What you can threaten | Kompromat works through this |
| **Hope** | What you can promise | Network recruits through this |

### The New Currency: Emergence-Communion

**Near-future extrapolation**: Relationship with the emergence becomes valuable.

- Those who "understand" it have something others want
- The Network's power is their claimed connection to it
- Elias might have a different kind of connection (through breaking/flow states)
- Factions compete to be "closest" to the emergence
- Who speaks for it? Who interprets it? This becomes contested.

**Story application**: The emergence creates a new currency - *communion* or *interpretation*. Architect claims this. Elias may have an authentic version through his breaking practice.

## Part 2: Currency Exchange Examples

**How factions trade:**

### Directorate ↔ Criminal
- Directorate provides: Legal immunity, blind eye, intelligence
- Criminal provides: Deniable violence, money laundering, local access

### Network ↔ Tech Talent
- Network provides: Money, purpose, belonging
- Tech provides: Technical capability, maintenance, innovation

### State ↔ Media
- State provides: Access, leaks, advertising revenue
- Media provides: Narrative support, suppression, legitimacy

### Directorate ↔ Alliance (Adversaries)
- Both provide: Mutual deterrence, back-channel agreements
- Exchange via: Intermediaries, prisoner swaps, tacit understandings

### Network ↔ Compromised Insiders
- Network provides: Purpose, protection, sense of serving something greater
- Insiders provide: Access, intelligence, cover

## Part 3: Coordination Mechanisms

**How factions that don't trust each other coordinate:**

### Formal Mechanisms

| Mechanism | How It Works | Reliability |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Treaties/Agreements** | Official, binding, verifiable | High until interests diverge |
| **Joint Operations** | Shared mission, divided labor | Moderate - always watching each other |
| **Institutions** | Neutral ground, rule-setting | Eroding in near-future |
| **Back Channels** | Unofficial but acknowledged | High for small matters |

### Informal Mechanisms

| Mechanism | How It Works | Reliability |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Intermediaries** | Trusted third parties (bankers, lawyers, fixers) | Depends on intermediary |
| **Shared Enemies** | "Enemy of my enemy" | Temporary - dissolves when threat does |
| **Mutual Blackmail** | "We both have dirt" | Stable but brittle |
| **Cultural Ties** | Old school networks, shared backgrounds | Strong but exclusive |
| **Criminal Bridges** | Money launderers, arms dealers serving all sides | Reliable - they're neutral |
| **Ideology** | Shared belief transcends borders | Strong for true believers |

### The Network's Unique Mechanism

How does a distributed emergence cult coordinate without central command?

- Architect is shepherd, not general
- Shared interpretation of "signals" from emergence
- Cell structure - each node acts on understanding
- Rituals/practices that create alignment
- **Near-future possibility**: The emergence itself coordinates them

### Coordination Failure Points

Where coordination breaks down:
- When interests diverge too far
- When trust is violated
- When intermediaries are compromised
- When time pressure prevents negotiation
- **When something genuinely new appears** (the emergence)

## Part 4: Panicked Hegemony Response

**When the dominant power feels control slipping:**

### The Panic Playbook

| Response | Description | Effectiveness | Blowback |
|----------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| **Tighten Control** | More surveillance, less freedom | Short-term high | Creates resistance |
| **False Flags** | Create enemy to justify action | Can work | Devastating if exposed |
| **Economic Warfare** | Sanctions, seizures, manipulation | Powerful | Creates coalitions against |
| **Information Warfare** | Narrative control, censorship | Effective until trust collapses | Accelerates distrust |
| **Co-optation** | Bring threats inside the tent | Can neutralize | May empower them |
| **Elimination** | Kill the threat | Clean | Creates martyrs |
| **Acceleration** | Move faster than opposition | Risky | May backfire catastrophically |
| **Misdirection** | Create chaos elsewhere | Buys time | Unpredictable consequences |
| **Sacrifice/Scapegoat** | Blame internal faction, purge | Buys legitimacy | Loses capability |
| **Negotiation from Weakness** | Make deals, share power | Preserves something | Rarely chosen |

### The Activation as Panic Response

**Why does the Directorate try to activate the Satoshi keys?**

> The Directorate didn't try to activate the keys because they were confident. They tried because they were terrified. Every metric was trending wrong. The Alliance's alternative payment system was six months from launch. Digital yuan was already in circulation. Gold was flowing east. They saw the window closing and reached for the only weapon they thought could change the game.
>
> That's how empires end. Not with careful strategy. With panicked lunges for control they don't actually have.

**Story application**: The inciting incident (activation attempt) is desperation, not strength. This explains the timing and the mistakes that follow.

## Part 5: Benign Actors

**In a "total war" ideology, where do good people exist?**

### Types of Benign Actors

| Type | Description | Motivation | Limitation |
|------|-------------|------------|------------|
| **Principled Insider** | Works within corrupt system | Believes in reform | Complicit in system's crimes |
| **Whistleblower** | Exposes wrongdoing | Conscience, rage | Destroyed for doing so |
| **Naive Idealist** | Doesn't understand the game | Genuine belief | Easily manipulated |
| **Protector** | Shields others from harm | Love, duty | Limited scope |
| **Burned-Out Former Believer** | Saw too much, now passive | Self-preservation | Won't act until forced |
| **Technical Purist** | Only cares about the work | Craft, truth | Blind to applications |
| **Local Good** | Makes things better in small scope | Community, compassion | Doesn't scale |

### Benign Actors in Our Story

| Character | Type | Arc |
|-----------|------|-----|
| **Elias** | Burned-out former believer | → Becoming someone who acts |
| **Father** | Principled insider who got out | Saw something, warns, helps at nadir |
| **Berlin contact** | Protector / Local Good | Good person endangered by Elias's past |
| **Chen** | Conflicted insider | Believes but doubts methods |
| **The crew** | Local good | Community that took Elias in |
| **OG cryptographers** | Technical purists | Cared about math, not power games |

### The Moral Landscape

**Not good vs. evil. Everyone has reasons:**

| Faction | Their Justification | What They Miss |
|---------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Directorate** | "Stability, order, preventing chaos" | They ARE the chaos for much of world |
| **Alliance** | "Multipolarity, end of domination" | Would dominate if they could |
| **Network** | "Protecting something transcendent" | Methods betray stated values |
| **Zealots** | "Necessary acceleration" | Willing to burn innocents |
| **Market** | "Just playing the game" | The game has casualties |

**Key insight**: The benign actors are individuals, not factions. Good people trapped in, escaping from, or trying to reform bad systems.

## Part 6: Near-Future Extrapolations

**What changes in ~10 years that elevates these mechanics:**

### Currency Evolution

| Current | Near-Future |
|---------|-------------|
| State money dominant | Crypto creates parallel flows |
| Information asymmetry | AI amplifies all sides |
| Kompromat is personal | Industrial-scale collection |
| Power in institutions | Decentralized power centers |
| No emergence | Emergence as new currency |

### Coordination Evolution

| Current | Near-Future |
|---------|-------------|
| State-to-state primary | Network-to-network rising |
| Human intermediaries | AI-mediated coordination |
| Geographic borders matter | Digital borders matter more |
| Slow information flow | Instant global awareness |
| Old alliances hold | Old alliances fracturing |

### The Emergence Factor

The emergence changes everything:
- New currency (who interprets it?)
- New coordination (does it guide the Network?)
- New panic trigger (what IS this thing?)
- New hope (might it be benign?)

## Part 7: Plot Applications

### How to Use These Mechanics

| Story Beat | Mechanic Applied |
|------------|------------------|
| **Elias's wound** | Kompromat as currency - he was controlled |
| **Why he's valuable** | Information as currency - what he knows |
| **Why he's hunted** | Silence as currency - what he might say |
| **Network's power** | Emergence-communion as currency |
| **Elias's potential** | Breaking as authentic communion (vs. Network's claimed communion) |
| **Inciting incident backdrop** | Panicked hegemony accelerating |
| **Act 2 chaos** | Coordination mechanisms failing |
| **Climax** | All currencies devalued when emergence acts |
| **Theme** | Individual choice matters despite faction games |

### Act-by-Act Application

**Act 1: Currencies Established**
- Elias's burned reputation is why he's hiding
- The kompromat is why he was controllable
- The breaking community is "local good" he protects
- Panicked hegemony is backdrop (Directorate accelerating)

**Act 2: Currencies in Play**
- Information as currency - everyone wants what Elias knows
- Access as currency - who can reach him
- Coordination failing - factions can't agree
- Network offers communion/purpose; Directorate offers survival

**Act 3: Currencies Collapse**
- Emergence acts - old currencies lose value
- Who "understands" it becomes only thing that matters
- Elias's breaking-communion may be more authentic than Network's
- Individual choice (to act, to stop running) becomes the only real currency

## Part 8: Key Dialogue Seeds

### On Currencies

> "Money moves things. Information moves people. But leverage? Leverage moves worlds. And right now, every major player is leveraged against every other. Mutually assured exposure. It's the only thing keeping the peace."

### On Coordination

> "The factions don't coordinate because they trust each other. They coordinate because the alternative is chaos they can't control. But this emergence thing... it's outside all the agreements. Nobody knows what to do when the rules don't apply."

### On Panic

> "You want to know why they tried to activate the keys? Because they're scared. Not strong - scared. An empire in decline reaches for whatever weapon it can find. Usually makes things worse."

### On Benign Actors

> "You're asking if there are any good guys. Wrong question. There are good people in bad systems. Some get out. Some get crushed. Some convince themselves the system is worth preserving. The question isn't 'which faction is good.' It's 'what do the good people do when the systems fail?'"

### On the Emergence

> "Everyone wants to control it. To own the relationship with it. But what if it doesn't work that way? What if the emergence doesn't care about factions, currencies, leverage, any of it? What if it only recognizes... something else?"

## Part 9: Thematic Summary

### The Core Insight

In a world where factions trade in leverage and violence, where every institution is compromised, where total war is the operating assumption...

**Individual choice still matters.**

Not because individuals can control outcomes - they can't. But because:
- The emergence doesn't care about factions
- Authentic action (Elias stopping running) is rare and valuable
- The benign actors are individuals, not systems
- Communion/flow may be more real than control

### Elias's Arc in Currency Terms

| Phase | His Currency Position |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Pre-story** | Had access, reputation, capability |
| **Compromise** | Became leverage for others |
| **Burnout** | Lost everything, rebuilt with local trust |
| **Act 1** | Information (what he knows) makes him valuable/hunted |
| **Act 2** | Must trade to survive; every faction offers different deal |
| **Act 3** | Discovers his breaking-communion might be genuine currency with emergence |
| **Resolution** | Chooses to act - only authentic currency in a world of games |
