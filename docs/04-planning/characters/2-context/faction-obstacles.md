# Faction Obstacles

## Overview

Each faction presents different types of conflict and opposing force. This document maps the obstacles, pressures, and threats each faction can deploy against Elias throughout the story.

---

| Faction | Primary Conflict | Secondary Conflict | Tertiary Conflict |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Network** | Ideological | Social/Reputational | Information |
| **Directorate** | Institutional | Physical | Legal |
| **Alliance** | Geopolitical | Physical | Extraction |

| **Market** | Financial | Informational | Mercenary |

## The Network (Crypto True Believers)

### Conflict Type: Ideological / Social

The Network doesn't have guns or arrest warrants. They have belief—and the infrastructure that belief built.

### Obstacles They Can Present

| Obstacle | Description | Story Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Reputation destruction** | Doxxing, social media campaigns, "he's a fed" accusations | Elias can't use crypto infrastructure without being identified and shunned |
| **Infrastructure denial** | Exchanges freeze accounts, nodes refuse transactions | His ability to trace patterns is blocked |
| **Information warfare** | Disinformation about his intentions, forged evidence | Allies become suspicious, can't verify who to trust |
| **Ideological purity tests** | Demands he prove he's "one of them" | Forced to take actions against his interests or ethics |
| **Hive mind pursuit** | Thousands of amateur sleuths tracking him | Can't stay hidden; crowdsourced surveillance |

### Pressure Mechanisms

```
LOW PRESSURE
├── Social media accusations
├── Frozen exchange accounts
└── Refused service at crypto-friendly venues

MEDIUM PRESSURE
├── Doxxing: real name, location published
├── False flag: framed as informant/government agent
└── Bounty: reward posted for information

HIGH PRESSURE
├── Physical confrontation by true believers
├── Complete infrastructure lockout
└── Swatting or real-world harassment campaigns
```

### Scene Possibilities

- Elias needs to access a mixing service; operator recognizes him from Network alerts
- Someone he trusted sells his location for crypto bounty
- A true believer confronts him: "You built the cage. You don't get to complain about the bars."

## The Directorate (US Government/Intelligence)

### Conflict Type: Institutional / Physical / Legal

The Directorate has the full weight of state power—but also bureaucracy, competing interests, and the need for deniability.

### Obstacles They Can Present

| Obstacle | Description | Story Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Legal jeopardy** | Warrants, indictments, international requests | Can't travel openly, use official channels |
| **Asset activation** | People Elias knows are actually assets | Betrayal from "safe" contacts |
| **Surveillance state** | Facial recognition, phone tracking, SIGINT | Every digital/physical movement tracked |
| **Rendition threat** | Black sites, off-book detention | Physical danger without legal recourse |
| **Institutional gaslighting** | Official denials, records altered, "you never worked here" | Can't prove his own history |
| **Kompromat deployment** | Releasing/threatening to release his secrets | Leverage at critical moments |

### Pressure Mechanisms

```
LOW PRESSURE
├── Surveillance: knows he's being watched
├── Frozen accounts: official financial lockout
└── Travel flags: can't cross borders easily

MEDIUM PRESSURE
├── Active pursuit: field teams deployed
├── Asset betrayal: trusted contact is handler
└── Legal threats: sealed indictment leverage

HIGH PRESSURE
├── Rendition attempt: black bag operation
├── Kompromat release: public destruction
└── "Accident" arrangement: deniable elimination
```

### Scene Possibilities

- Elias realizes his Berlin contact reports to Langley
- A sealed indictment surfaces: he can cooperate or be extradited
- Someone he knew from Prometheus warns him: "They're not trying to bring you in anymore"

## The Alliance (BRICS Bloc)

### Conflict Type: Geopolitical / Extraction

The Alliance wants what Elias knows, and they have the resources of nation-states behind them—just different nation-states.

### Obstacles They Can Present

| Obstacle | Description | Story Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Extraction pressure** | Offers of asylum that become demands | "Come willingly or we'll arrange transport" |
| **Honey traps (redux)** | Operatives deployed to compromise him again | Trust issues amplified |
| **Proxy operations** | Criminal elements contracted for deniability | Violent actors with state backing |
| **Economic leverage** | Pressure on anyone helping him through BRICS-aligned channels | His support network squeezed |
| **Information warfare** | Leaking his secrets to damage Western operations | Collateral damage to people he cares about |

### Pressure Mechanisms

```
LOW PRESSURE
├── Approach: friendly offer of sanctuary
├── Information: demonstrate they know everything
└── Incentive: money, safety, resources

MEDIUM PRESSURE
├── Threat: family/contacts in their reach
├── Proxy violence: criminal contractors
└── Exposure: threaten to leak what they know

HIGH PRESSURE
├── Snatch operation: forced extraction
├── Leverage harm: hurt someone to compel cooperation
└── False flag: frame him for something to force his hand
```

### Scene Possibilities

- An Alliance operative approaches in Berlin: "We can protect you. The Americans can't."
- Mira (if in story) is being used as intermediary—is she still working for them?
- Elias's father receives a visitor with "concerns about your son's safety"

## The Zealots (ARCHITECT's Cult)

### Conflict Type: Psychological / Spiritual / Physical

The Zealots don't want to stop Elias or capture him. They want to *convert* him. Or, failing that, fulfill their prophecy through him whether he consents or not.

### Obstacles They Can Present

| Obstacle | Description | Story Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Ideological seduction** | ARCHITECT offers meaning, purpose, understanding | Temptation to believe, belong |
| **Prophecy trap** | Elias is cast in their narrative; his actions "fulfill" their beliefs | Can't act without serving their story |
| **True believer interference** | Cult members helping him in ways he didn't ask for | Unwanted allies with their own agenda |
| **Psychological warfare** | They know his wounds; ARCHITECT mirrors his journey | Identity destabilization |
| **Ritual requirements** | They need him for something specific | Object, not agent |
| **Violence as sacrament** | Killing for the emergence is holy to them | Unpredictable, ideologically-motivated violence |

### Pressure Mechanisms

```
LOW PRESSURE
├── Contact: messages that demonstrate understanding
├── Assistance: help he didn't ask for, debts created
└── Interpretation: everything he does is "prophesied"

MEDIUM PRESSURE
├── Confrontation: ARCHITECT appears, offers dialogue
├── Entrapment: situations where only Zealots can help
└── Isolation: other factions pushed away, only Zealots remain

HIGH PRESSURE
├── Forced participation: drugged, coerced into ritual
├── Sacrifice demand: someone he loves at risk
└── Revelation: ARCHITECT knows truth about emergence (or claims to)
```

### Scene Possibilities

- A Zealot saves Elias's life—then expects spiritual payment
- ARCHITECT and Elias meet: "We both saw the patterns, Elias. I just didn't run."
- Elias finds himself at a ceremony; they've been expecting him

## The Market (Financial Players)

### Conflict Type: Financial / Mercenary / Informational

The Market doesn't have ideology. It has interests. Elias is valuable—as asset, as information source, as liability to be managed.

### Obstacles They Can Present

| Obstacle | Description | Story Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Financial strangulation** | Every account frozen, every transaction flagged | Can't buy food, shelter, transport |
| **Mercenary deployment** | Private military contractors, corporate security | Professional violence for hire |
| **Information bounties** | Rewards for his location, knowledge, cooperation | Everyone becomes potential betrayer |
| **Market manipulation** | Actions taken to force his hand through financial pressure | Indirect coercion |
| **Acquisition attempts** | Offers to buy his cooperation at any price | Temptation through resources |
| **Liability elimination** | If he can't be bought, he's a risk to be removed | Clean, professional, businesslike |

### Pressure Mechanisms

```
LOW PRESSURE
├── Offers: increasingly generous buyout proposals
├── Surveillance: private intelligence gathering
└── Leverage: financial pressure on associates

MEDIUM PRESSURE
├── Contractors: professional surveillance/pursuit teams
├── Freezing: coordinated financial lockout
└── Bidding war: multiple parties competing for his cooperation

HIGH PRESSURE
├── Acquisition by force: kidnapping for debriefing
├── Elimination contract: he's worth more gone than free
└── Scorched earth: destroy everything he values to force terms
```

### Scene Possibilities

- A hedge fund manager offers Elias more money than he's ever seen: "Just tell us what the patterns mean"
- Private contractors corner him: "Our client wants a conversation. Please don't make this difficult."
- He discovers the Market has been funding both the Zealots and the Directorate's pursuit

## The Emergence (The Phenomenon)

### Conflict Type: Existential / Perceptual / Unknown

The Emergence isn't a faction in the traditional sense. It's a force—possibly intelligent, possibly not—that creates obstacles simply by existing.

### Obstacles It Can Present

| Obstacle | Description | Story Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Perceptual unreliability** | Elias can't tell if he's seeing patterns or imagining them | Self-doubt at critical moments |
| **Communication failure** | It might be trying to tell him something; he can't understand | Frustration, misinterpretation |
| **Unintended consequences** | His interactions with it cause effects he didn't predict | Playing with forces beyond control |
| **Attention cost** | Seeing the patterns takes a toll—mental, physical | Degradation over time |
| **Other seekers** | He's not the only one it's "talking" to | Competition, comparison |
| **Choice demands** | It seems to want something from him; he doesn't know what | Pressure without clarity |

### Pressure Mechanisms

```
LOW PRESSURE
├── Patterns: appearing more frequently, demanding attention
├── Synchronicities: "coincidences" that can't be coincidence
└── Dreams: information coming through unconscious channels

MEDIUM PRESSURE
├── Physical effects: headaches, nosebleeds, dissociation when patterns intensify
├── Isolation: others can't see what he sees, alienation
└── Compulsion: feeling pulled toward locations, actions

HIGH PRESSURE
├── Breakdown risk: the patterns threaten his sanity
├── Choice point: the emergence demands action he doesn't understand
└── Integration or dissolution: become part of it or be destroyed by it
```

### Scene Possibilities

- Elias sees the pattern clearly for the first time—and has a seizure
- The emergence moves the keys in response to something Elias did; he doesn't know what
- At the climax: the emergence offers him understanding, but at the cost of his individual identity

## Cross-Faction Obstacle Combinations

The most dangerous moments come when factions' obstacles compound:

| Combination | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| **Directorate + Market** | Legal jeopardy + financial lockout = no legal or economic options |
| **Network + Zealots** | Doxxed to true believers who see him as prophet or antichrist |
| **Alliance + Directorate** | Both state actors pursuing, nowhere is safe |
| **Market + Zealots** | Money funding religious violence |
| **Emergence + Any** | Perceptual unreliability undermines response to any threat |

### Peak Pressure Scenario (Act 2 End/Act 3 Start)

```
ELIAS'S SITUATION:
├── Network: Doxxed, bounty on his location
├── Directorate: Active pursuit, sealed indictment, kompromat ready
├── Alliance: Father has been "visited"
├── Zealots: ARCHITECT claims to have answers, demands meeting
├── Market: Contractors closing in, all accounts frozen
└── Emergence: Patterns intensifying, physical symptoms mounting

AVAILABLE OPTIONS: None obvious
REQUIRED: Alliance of necessity, choice that closes other doors
```

## Using Obstacles in Scene Construction

Each obstacle should:

1. **Force a choice** — Elias can't just endure; he must act
2. **Have costs** — Every solution creates new problems
3. **Reveal character** — How he responds shows who he is
4. **Advance plot** — Obstacles push toward climax, not just delay it
5. **Connect to theme** — Control vs. emergence, trust vs. isolation

### Obstacle Scene Template

```
SETUP: Faction applies pressure
CHOICE: Elias must respond
OPTIONS:
├── A: Solves immediate problem, creates new faction enemy
├── B: Avoids confrontation, loses resource/ally/time
└── C: Unconventional solution, costs something personal

AFTERMATH: New status quo, new obstacles enabled
```
