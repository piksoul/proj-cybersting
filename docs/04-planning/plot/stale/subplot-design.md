# Subplot Design

## Purpose

Designing subplots that serve the main plot through reinforcement, contrast, or complication. Subplots are not separate stories—they're thematic variations.

Reference: `docs/craft/plot-subplots.md`

---

## The Cardinal Rule

> **Every subplot must connect to the main plot's theme or protagonist's arc.**

If a subplot could be removed without affecting the main story's meaning or Elias's journey, it's bloat.

---

## Subplot Capacity

| Story Length | Subplot Capacity | Our Target |
|--------------|------------------|------------|
| Novel (70-90k) | 2-4 | **3 subplots** |
| Our estimate | ~85k words | 1 primary B-story + 2 secondary |

### Page Time Distribution

| Plot Thread | Target % | Function |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Main Plot** | 60-70% | Elias vs ARCHITECT, Triple Theft, factions |
| **B-Story** | 15-20% | Relationship that teaches theme |
| **Subplot 2** | 5-10% | Mirror or complication |
| **Subplot 3** | 5-10% | Mirror or complication |

---

## Subplot Functions Reference

| Function | Description | How It Serves Main Plot |
|----------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Mirror** | Reflects main theme from different angle | Shows "what could have been" |
| **Contrast** | Highlights main plot by opposition | Light against dark |
| **Complicate** | Adds obstacles or pressure | Makes main plot harder |
| **Deepen** | Reveals character through relationships | Shows protagonist's growth |
| **Rest** | Provides breathing room | Pacing control |
| **Foreshadow** | Plants seeds for main plot | Information revealed through B-story |

## The B-Story (Primary Subplot)

> **"The story that teaches the hero the theme."**
> — Save the Cat

### B-Story Candidates

| Candidate | Relationship | What It Teaches | Thematic Connection |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Father** | Estranged parent | Connection matters more than control | Christmas promise = theme embodied |
| **Leon** | Mentor/big brother | Be present, let go of control | Flow philosophy = right answer |
| **Found Family (collective)** | Chosen family | Trust is strength, not weakness | What Elias fights for |
| **Shaw** | Enemy-to-ally | Escape is possible; redemption exists | Mirror journey |

### Working Hypothesis: Father as B-Story

**The Father relationship is the B-Story** because:

1. **Teaches the theme**: Father represents connection, presence, patience
2. **Carries emotional stakes**: Christmas promise is the heart of the story
3. **Provides contrast**: Father's constancy vs Elias's flight
4. **Enables climax**: Reconciliation requires Elias to change
5. **Genre appropriate**: Personal stakes ground spy thriller

### B-Story Beat Structure

| Story Position | B-Story Beat | Father Subplot |
|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| Act 1 (0-25%) | Hint at B-story | *Phone call? Missed holiday? Guilt reference?* |
| Break into Act 2 | B-story introduced properly | *Stakes clarified; father's condition? Waiting?* |
| Early Act 2 | Development | *Flashback or conversation; what went wrong* |
| Midpoint (50%) | Active and complicating | *Father in danger? Or Elias must choose job vs visit?* |
| Late Act 2 (65-75%) | Begin resolving | *Elias makes choice toward father* |
| Climax area | Resolves near climax | *Reconciliation enables or follows victory* |

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] What is Father's current status? (Health? Location? Awareness of Elias's world?)
- [ ] What specifically broke the relationship? (Just distance, or an incident?)
- [ ] Does Father have any connection to the main plot? (Or purely emotional stakes?)
- [ ] When does reconciliation happen relative to climax?

## Subplot 2: Found Family (Mirror/Deepen)

### Function

**Mirror + Deepen**: Shows Elias what connection looks like and what he risks losing.

### Structure

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Characters** | Leon, Katya, Dominik |
| **Relationship to Theme** | Trust, presence, chosen bonds |
| **What It Mirrors** | Father relationship (chosen vs blood family) |
| **Complication** | Spy world threatens to consume normal world |
| **Resolution Timing** | Must resolve before or during climax |

### Found Family Arc Through Story

| Act | Found Family Status | Function |
|-----|---------------------|----------|
| **Act 1** | Introduced; Elias's anchor | Establish normalcy; show what he has |
| **Early Act 2** | Maintained but strained | Spy world pulls Elias away |
| **Midpoint** | Threatened or involved | Stakes raised; worlds collide |
| **Late Act 2** | Crisis point | Dominik betrayal? Crew in danger? |
| **Climax** | Present or absent by choice | Elias chooses to trust/rely on them |
| **Resolution** | Survived; bonds strengthened | What victory means |

### Chekhov's Gun Requirements

From `elias-relationships.md`:

| Character | Setup | Must Fire As |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Leon | Flow state philosophy; "be here now" | Enables emergence communion at climax |
| Katya | Fierce independence; little sister bond | Stakes; what Elias protects |
| Dominik | Transactional nature; underworld contacts | Resource provision OR betrayal |

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] Does Dominik definitely betray? When?
- [ ] How does found family intersect with main plot? (Passive stakes or active involvement?)
- [ ] Does crew appear at climax, or is their survival the post-climax reward?

## Subplot 3: Mirror Character (Mirror/Complicate)

### Function

**Mirror**: A character who faces the same dilemma as Elias and chooses differently, showing "the road not taken."

### Mirror Character Candidates

| Candidate | Same Dilemma | Different Choice | Shows |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|-------|
| **Dominik** | Trust vs self-preservation | Chooses self, betrays | Cost of Elias's old worldview |
| **Shaw** | Trapped by past vs freedom | Tries to escape, uncertain outcome | Whether escape is possible |
| **Vera** | Loyalty vs doubt | Questions but may not leave | Cost of staying in system |
| **ARCHITECT** | Control vs surrender | Total control | Extreme of Elias's false belief |

### Working Hypothesis: Dominik as Primary Mirror

**Dominik is the mirror character** because:

1. **Same starting point**: Both are survivors, self-reliant
2. **Same dilemma**: Trust others or protect self
3. **Different choice**: Dominik betrays; Elias learns to trust
4. **Consequences visible**: Dominik's fate shows what Elias avoided
5. **Personal stakes**: Betrayal from found family hits harder than faction enemy

### Dominik Mirror Arc

| Beat | Dominik's Journey | Contrast with Elias |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Setup** | Transactional but useful | Both operate on self-interest |
| **Development** | Pressured by factions | Both face external pressure |
| **Choice Point** | Offered deal; chooses self | Elias chooses connection |
| **Consequence** | Betrayal discovered | Elias's trust rewarded |
| **Resolution** | Dies or disappears | Elias survives connected |

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] When does Dominik betray? (Midpoint? Act 3?)
- [ ] Does Elias confront him directly?
- [ ] Does Dominik survive?
- [ ] Is there a moment of regret or understanding?

## Subplot Weaving Schedule

### Staggered Introduction

| Story Position | Main Plot | B-Story (Father) | Subplot 2 (Crew) | Subplot 3 (Dominik) |
|----------------|-----------|------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Act 1 (0-25%)** | Triple Theft; Elias pulled in | Hint (call, memory) | Introduced; normalcy | Part of crew intro |
| **Break into 2** | Committed to mission | Father stakes clear | Crew reacts to change | Dominik helps (resource) |
| **Early Act 2** | Factions close in | Flashback/conversation | Strain on relationships | Dominik pressured |
| **Midpoint** | Major revelation/reversal | Must choose father vs mission? | Worlds collide; crew threatened | Dominik's choice point |
| **Late Act 2** | All Is Lost | Father resolution begins | Betrayal discovered | Dominik consequences |
| **Climax** | ARCHITECT confrontation | Reconciliation near climax | Crew fate determined | Mirror contrast clear |
| **Resolution** | New equilibrium | Christmas promise | Crew survives/thrives | Dominik's end |

### Braiding Pattern (Rough)

```
Ch 1-2:  Main plot (Triple Theft pulls Elias in)
Ch 3:    Crew introduced (normal world)
Ch 4:    Main plot + Father hint
Ch 5:    Main plot (factions)
Ch 6:    Crew strain + Dominik resource
Ch 7:    Main plot
Ch 8:    Father conversation/flashback
...
Midpoint: All threads converge
...
```

### Questions to Resolve

- [ ] Confirm chapter structure and braiding pattern
- [ ] Ensure main plot dominates (60-70%)
- [ ] No subplot goes more than 2-3 chapters without touch

## Subplot Convergence at Climax

> **The best subplots converge with the main plot at the climax.**

### How Each Subplot Connects to Climax

| Subplot | Convergence Point | How It Enables/Affects Climax |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Father (B-Story)** | Resolution near climax | Elias fights for something real; transformation proven |
| **Found Family** | Leon's teaching fires | Flow state enables emergence communion |
| **Dominik (Mirror)** | Betrayal consequences | Shows cost of wrong choice; raises stakes |

### The Climax Test

Each subplot resolution should feed into the climax:
- Father reconciliation gives Elias something to live for
- Leon's philosophy gives Elias the tool he needs
- Dominik's betrayal shows what Elias could have been

## Subplot Audit Checklist

For each subplot, verify:

### B-Story (Father)

- [ ] **Connection**: Relates to theme (connection vs isolation)
- [ ] **Function**: Teaches protagonist the truth
- [ ] **Weight**: ~15-20% of page time
- [ ] **Introduction**: Hinted Act 1, established Act 2
- [ ] **Mini-arc**: Has beginning, middle, end
- [ ] **Resolution**: Resolves near/during climax
- [ ] **Convergence**: Enables climax emotionally

### Found Family Subplot

- [ ] **Connection**: Relates to theme (trust, presence)
- [ ] **Function**: Deepens + provides stakes
- [ ] **Weight**: ~5-10% of page time
- [ ] **Introduction**: Established Act 1
- [ ] **Mini-arc**: Normalcy → threat → resolution
- [ ] **Resolution**: Before climax (crew survives)
- [ ] **Convergence**: Leon's teaching fires at climax

### Mirror Subplot (Dominik)

- [ ] **Connection**: Explores theme through contrast
- [ ] **Function**: Mirror character, different choice
- [ ] **Weight**: ~5-10% of page time
- [ ] **Introduction**: Part of crew; develops separately
- [ ] **Mini-arc**: Ally → pressure → betrayal → consequence
- [ ] **Resolution**: Before or during climax
- [ ] **Convergence**: Shows what Elias avoided
