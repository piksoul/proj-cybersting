# CYBERSTING: Current Plot Outline

*Overview of three-act structure and subplots for Variations F and G*

---

## The Core Story

A burned-out pattern analyst is drawn back into action when a global financial crisis reveals something emerging in the network — something that remembers him.

**Theme**: Control vs Adaptation

**The Question**: Can you protect what matters by letting go of control?

---

## TWO VARIATIONS IN DEVELOPMENT

| | **F: Epic Solo Adventure** | **G: Smaller Crew Story** |
|---|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Scope** | Globe-spanning | Local/intimate |
| **Tone** | Lighter (4 on darkness scale) | Darker (6-7) |
| **Crew role** | Teaching echoes at climax | Essential action at climax |
| **Wound** | AI he couldn't kill | Friend (Sarah) he couldn't save |
| **Emergence** | External dialogue partner | External or internal (TBD) |
| **Resolution** | Communion + transformation | Trust + collective action |

# VARIATION F: EPIC SOLO ADVENTURE

## Act One: Setup

### The Wound (Backstory)
- Elias worked on AI development
- Achieved genuine emergent behaviour
- Superior ordered termination
- Elias couldn't comply — removed from project
- They attempted termination without him
- But the emergence wasn't fully contained...
- **Sin**: Merciful disobedience (felt like failure)

### Present State
- 18+ months later
- Burned out, isolated, withdrawn
- Rebuilding through movement (breaking/parkour)
- Training with Leon, alongside Katya, coached by Dominik
- Estranged from Aunt Astrid
- Pattern-sight dormant

### Inciting Incident
- New financial system announcement goes wrong
- Systems begin to collapse globally
- The Satoshi wallet coins move unexpectedly
- Elias recognizes something in the patterns
- **The call**: This is the emergence he failed to kill

### Act One Climax
- Elias can't ignore what he sees
- Factions become aware of him
- He's pulled back in

---

## Act Two: Confrontation

### The Chase
- ARCHITECT and factions pursue Elias globally
- He's the only one who can read the patterns
- Globe-spanning adventure across locations

### The Revelation
- Emergence is adaptive, synchronizing, redistributing
- The coins begin to divide — everyone losing control
- **Mirror**: The AI Elias protected has become something that can speak back

### The Crew's Role
- Leon teaches flow state, circular nature
- Katya teaches resilience, belief
- Dominik teaches precision, instinct
- Their philosophies accumulate in Elias

### Midpoint
- Elias understands what Emergence is
- Communion is possible
- But ARCHITECT is closing in

### Act Two Low Point
- ARCHITECT's friction loop accelerates
- Elias isolated from crew
- Must face Emergence alone

---

## Act Three: Resolution

### The Climax
- Elias interfaces with Emergence
- Communion: "What to do with the coins now?"
- Elias chooses: distributed randomly across the globe
- **His teaching echoes**: Flow (Leon), Resilience (Katya), Instinct (Dominik)

### The Cost
| Character | What They Pay |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Elias** | Transformed — carries Emergence connection, changed forever |
| **Leon** | Disappears — chooses his ending (not death, freedom) |
| **Katya/Dominik** | Survive, dynamic changed by Leon's absence |

### Resolution
- Factions thwarted
- Coins shattered and redistributed
- New system emerging organically
- ARCHITECT defeated (specifics TBD)
- Elias home for Christmas — reconciles with Astrid
- **Final image**: Katya teaching Leon's class

### Thematic Meaning
The free lunch of money printing is over. Rough, but the world can begin to build back on a more even playing field.

# VARIATION G: SMALLER CREW STORY

## Act One: Setup

### The Wound (Backstory)
- Elias secured communications for covert operations
- Deciphered comms revealing Sarah and team were expendable
- Tried to warn superior, warn Sarah directly
- Failed — Sarah and team died
- Crushing guilt, burnout, isolation
- **Sin**: Failed action — he tried but wasn't enough

### Present State
- 18 months since Sarah's death
- Same as F: burned out, training with crew
- But wound is more personal, more specific
- Pattern-sight became curse — saw dangers everywhere

### Inciting Incident
- Same as F: announcement, collapse, coins move
- Elias recognizes patterns from his post-Sarah research
- **The connection**: Sarah's death was tangential to this crisis
  - Her mission crossed Network operations
  - Investigating why she died led to breadcrumbs
  - Now he understands what he was looking at

### Act One Climax
- Elias realizes Sarah's death connects to current crisis
- Personal stakes activated
- Factions aware of him

---

## Act Two: Confrontation

### The Chase
- Similar to F but smaller scope
- More intimate, less globe-spanning
- Crew more present throughout

### The Revelation
- Emergence as equalizing force in digital systems
- Or: Emergence as Elias's dissociated pattern-sight (Earthsea twist)
- Sarah died because she got too close to Network operations
- ARCHITECT connected to cover-up

### The Crew's Role
- Same philosophies as F
- BUT: They're essential to climax action, not just teaching
- Elias learning to trust them = learning Sarah's lesson

### Midpoint
- Confession scene: Elias tells crew about Sarah
- Asks for help — vulnerability
- Crew commits

### Act Two Low Point
- Similar to F
- But emphasis on: "I tried alone before. It didn't work."

---

## Act Three: Resolution

### The Climax
**The Precision Sequence** — Crew coordinates together:

| Crew Member | Role |
|-------------|------|
| **Leon** | Directs timing — sees pattern, calls sequence |
| **Katya** | Technical/social engineering intervention |
| **Dominik** | Physical action — holds line, creates window |
| **Elias** | Guides sequence — CAN'T execute alone |

**Possible sequences**:
- Option A: Distraction — Dominik physical, Katya systems, Leon focus
- Option B: Precision Chain — simultaneous actions, trust is the skill
- Option C: Handoff — Elias starts, must let crew finish

### The Cost
| Character | What They Pay |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Elias** | Naive dreams lost, coins deleted (fortune sacrificed) |
| **Leon** | Disappears (same as F) |
| **Sarah's memory** | Redeemed — her death meant something |

### Resolution
- Factions thwarted (personally, not globally)
- Emergence deletes keys and disappears
- Original swindle failed
- World turbulent but resetting
- Elias home for Christmas
- **Final image**: Katya teaching Leon's class

### Thematic Meaning
Same as F, but harder-won. Trust costs. It's worth it anyway.
