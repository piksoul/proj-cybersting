# Story Scale Framework

## Purpose

Before choosing Kompromat, Emergence, and Climax specifics, calibrate the *kind* of story you want to tell. Each scale represents a spectrum; your position on each will guide decisions.

---

## How to Use This

1. For each scale, mark where you want to land (1-10 or just "lean left/right")
2. See how your positions pattern together
3. Use the "Implications" to filter the decision options

---

## PATTERN RECOGNITION

Once you've marked your positions, look for clusters:

### If You're Mostly Left (1-4):
**Archetype**: Literary Thriller
- Profound, genre-respecting, thematically pure
- Earns the light, intimate, grounded
- Clear resolution, carries message
- **Model**: Le Carré meets Crichton

### If You're Mostly Middle (5-6):
**Archetype**: Crossover Thriller
- Balances depth and pace
- Genre-aware but not bound
- Rich without chaos
- **Model**: Neuromancer, The Spy Who Came In From the Cold

### If You're Mostly Right (7-10):
**Archetype**: Literary SF
- Fresh, messy, transcendent
- Elias as witness, ambiguous ending
- Mirror over message
- **Model**: Annihilation, 2001, Jeff VanderMeer

### Mixed Pattern:
Most interesting stories have **tension between scales**. For example:
- Roller coaster pace (7) + Profound theme (3) = Thrilling but meaningful
- Hero (3) + Chaos (8) = Hero faces incomprehensible
- Earn the Light (2) + Ambiguity (8) = Hopeful but open

## QUICK DECISION FILTER

Once you know your scale positions, filter the options:

### Kompromat
- Profound + Intimate → A (Oversight) or C (Confession)
- Roller Coaster + Epic → B (Market) or D (Kill Switch)
- Fresh + Transcendent → E (Pattern Sighting) or Combo 3

### Emergence
- Singularity + Grounded → A (Distributed)
- Chaos + Transcendent → C (New Being) or B (Adaptive)
- Balanced all around → Hybrid 1 (Adaptive Being)

### Climax
- Hero + Resolution → B (Trust Crew) or E (Complete)
- Witness + Ambiguity → D (Let Go)
- Hero + Transcendent → A (Communion) or Combo 1
