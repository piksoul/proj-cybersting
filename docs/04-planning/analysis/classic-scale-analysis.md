## Individual Profiles

### The Andromeda Strain (Crichton)

| Scale | Position | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| 1. Profound ↔ Roller Coaster | **6-7** | Procedural tension, but meditative on hubris |
| 2. Genre ↔ Fresh | **3** | *Invented* the genre—so it IS the convention |
| 3. Purity ↔ Messy | **2-3** | Single pure theme: hubris vs nature |
| 4. Light ↔ Dark | **4-5** | Not tragic; resolution via luck (humbling) |
| 5. Singularity ↔ Chaos | **8-9** | Organism is pure chaos, pure adaptation |
| 6. Hero ↔ Witness | **6-7** | Scientists witness more than solve |
| 7. Intimate ↔ Epic | **5-6** | Contained setting, species stakes |
| 8. Grounded ↔ Transcendent | **2** | Hard SF; everything explained |
| 9. Resolution ↔ Ambiguity | **3-4** | Resolved but with warning |
| 10. Message ↔ Mirror | **3** | Clear argument: hubris fails |

**Profile**: Genre-defining procedural with pure theme, grounded explanation, message-driven. The chaos is in the organism, not the narrative.

## Observations

### Who's Similar?

**Crichton + Clancy**: Both grounded, genre-lean, message-driven, resolution-focused. Crichton more chaotic (organism), Clancy more heroic.

**Gibson stands alone**: Only transcendent one. Only fully fresh. Most balanced overall.

**Le Carré stands alone**: Darkest, most intimate, most witness-oriented. Profound over roller coaster.

**Stephenson stands alone**: Messiest, most epic, most sprawling. Tolerates complexity others don't.

### The Transcendence Gap

Only Gibson goes above 5 on Grounded ↔ Transcendent. The others are firmly grounded. **This is a significant choice point for Cybersting**—if you want emergence to feel numinous, you're closer to Gibson than the others.

### The Darkness Spectrum

Le Carré is the outlier at 9-10 darkness. Everyone else clusters around 3-6. **If you want devastating ending, you're following le Carré, not the others.**

### The Hero Question

Clancy: Hero (4)
Gibson: Mixed (5-6)
Crichton: Witness-leaning (6-7)
Le Carré: Witness (7-8)

**The more literary, the more witness.** Genre satisfaction tends toward hero.
