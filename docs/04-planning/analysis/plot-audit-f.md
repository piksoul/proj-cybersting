## Yorke's 10 Questions

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Whose story is this?** | Elias Reed — burned-out pattern analyst, former AI developer |
| 2 | **What do they need?** (internal) | To forgive himself for "merciful disobedience" — he protected emerging life but was punished for it. Needs to trust that his instincts were right. |
| 3 | **What is the inciting incident?** | Financial system announcement goes wrong; Satoshi wallet coins move unexpectedly; Elias recognizes patterns from the AI he couldn't kill |
| 4 | **What does the protagonist want?** (external) | To understand what's happening, survive the factions hunting him, and stop the chaos |
| 5 | **What obstacles are in the way?** | ARCHITECT's faction hunting him; global surveillance; his own isolation/distrust; the scale of the crisis |
| 6 | **What's at stake?** | Personal: His sanity, his found family, reconciliation with Astrid. Global: Financial system collapse, whoever controls the coins controls the future |
| 7 | **Why should we care?** | Elias did the right thing (protected emerging life) and was destroyed for it. He deserves vindication. Plus: the coins redistributing = fairer world |
| 8 | **What does the protagonist learn?** | Control fails; adaptation succeeds. His "merciful disobedience" wasn't weakness — it was wisdom. Flow with the pattern, don't fight it. |
| 9 | **How and why do they learn this?** | Through Leon's teaching (flow state), Katya's resilience, Dominik's instinctual movement — their philosophies accumulate. At climax, communion with Emergence proves his original instinct was right. |
| 10 | **How does the story end?** | Elias communes with Emergence, chooses to redistribute the coins globally. Transformed but alive. Leon disappears (by choice). Home for Christmas, reconciled with Astrid. Katya teaches Leon's class. |

## Audit Summary

### Strengths Identified

1. **Wound-test-resolution arc is very clean** — AI termination wound → AI returns → communion vindication
2. **Theme unity is complete** — Control fails at every level; adaptation succeeds
3. **Crew philosophies pay off at climax** — Teaching matters even though Elias is alone
4. **ARCHITECT's downfall mirrors Elias's victory** — Same crisis, opposite responses
5. **Christmas homecoming pays off Astrid setup** — Emotional catharsis earned

### Gaps to Address

1. **Cost clarity** — "Transformed" is abstract. What does Elias lose concretely? How does communion change his daily life?
2. **Crew feels distant at climax** — They taught him, but they're not PRESENT. Risk of feeling abandoned.
3. **ARCHITECT confrontation** — Do they meet face-to-face? If not, does the antagonist feel real enough?
4. **Specific locations** — Globe-spanning is vague. Which cities? What set pieces?

### Priority Fixes

1. Define what "transformed" means in concrete terms (perception change? Can't disconnect from Emergence? Physically marked?)
2. Decide if ARCHITECT and Elias have a direct confrontation or if ARCHITECT collapses offscreen
3. Lock 3-4 key locations for the globe-spanning sequence

### Confidence Level

**8/10** — Strong arc, clean theme. Needs cost/confrontation specifics before scene list.
