# World Bible

Reference for Promptinator scene generation. Everything here applies globally unless a scene explicitly overrides it.

---

## ERA & SETTING — LOCKED (core) / UNLOCKED (specifics)

**When:** Near-future. Not dated precisely — close enough to feel plausible, far enough to permit speculative tech. Think 5-10 years from now. Avoid pinning a specific year unless narratively required.

**Where:** Global. Primary locations span the U.S. (institutional power), Europe (chase/subculture), and international flashpoints. The story moves — it doesn't stay in one city.

**Feel:** The world is recognisable but slightly worse. Surveillance is ambient, not dystopian. Corporate infrastructure is fortress-like. Street level clings to analogue as a form of resistance. Technology is unevenly distributed — state actors have capabilities individuals don't, but the network itself is beyond any single actor's control.

**Key atmospheric rules:**
- Cities at night are the default palette — neon, sodium, LED, cold air
- Institutional spaces are sterile, humming, fluorescent — power expressed as boredom
- Subculture spaces are warm, loud, human — resistance expressed as presence
- The natural world appears rarely and pointedly — when it does, it's significant

## TECHNOLOGY — LOCKED (constraints) / UNLOCKED (specifics)

### Guiding principle

Speculative but grounded. Every piece of tech should feel like something that exists today but slightly better, or something plausibly in development. No sci-fi overstretch. If a reader Googles it, they should find a real-world analogue.

### Cryptographic infrastructure

- **HSMs (Hardware Security Modules):** Physical devices that store and operate on cryptographic keys without exposing them. Used in the SCIF key ceremony. Real technology, real procedures.
- **Multi-signature (multi-sig):** Keys split into shards; multiple shards required to sign. The Satoshi keys use this. Real Bitcoin capability.
- **Shamir Secret Sharing:** Mathematical method for splitting a secret into parts. The "real key" may involve Shamir shares (Ch19 — multi-layered: cryptographic + biological + narrative).
- **Key ceremonies:** Formal, witnessed procedures for activating or rotating cryptographic keys. Highly ritualised in real life. In the story, the ceremony's failure is the inciting incident.
- **Quantum-resistant signing modules:** Referenced as near-future tech. Not central to the plot but part of the world's tech texture.

### Surveillance & security

- **Ambient surveillance:** Sensors, cameras, biometric scanners are everywhere in institutional and urban spaces. Not totalitarian — more like weather. You live with it.
- **Drone curtains:** Automated drone formations scanning biometrics at scale. Used at borders, airports, high-security perimeters.
- **Spoofed biometrics / facial-morph camouflage:** Countermeasures exist but are expensive and unreliable. Going off-grid is hard, not impossible.
- **Digital shadows:** Persistent data trails that track individuals across networks. Characters are aware of them. Escaping your digital shadow is a plot-level achievement, not a background assumption.

### Network & AI

- **Rogue nodes / governance handshakes:** The Bitcoin network shows signs of unauthorised coordination (Ch9). This is ambiguous — could be human-operated or emergent.
- **Cached AI agents:** AI systems that communicate in sub-symbolic languages. Background texture, not central plot tech. They exist in the world's infrastructure.
- **AI-assisted forensic blockchain analysis:** State-level tool for tracing transactions. Elias has experience with this from his NSA days.
- **The emergent entity:** Whatever controls the Satoshi keys exhibits behaviour that could be interpreted as AI, collective human action, or network emergence. The world bible does not resolve this — see character bible under "The Saboteur."

### Communications

- **Darknet comms:** Eerie, minimal UIs. Functional, not stylish. Used by dissidents, criminals, and intelligence operatives interchangeably.
- **Encrypted messaging:** Standard for anyone with something to hide. Not remarkable in this world.
- **Off-grid communication:** Difficult. Requires physical proximity, dead drops, or legacy infrastructure (radio, physical media). Going off-grid is a story event, not a convenience.

## GEOPOLITICAL BACKDROP — LOCKED

### The monetary landscape

The world is in a slow-motion monetary crisis. Multiple power blocs are manoeuvring for advantage:

- **United States:** Secretly holds ~1.3M BTC (the Satoshi wallets). Plans a controlled monetary reset — dump gold, crash emerging-market reserves, reveal BTC holdings, transition to a Bitcoin-backed reserve hybrid. This is the "New Bretton Woods" — designed to secure U.S. dominance for a century.
- **BRICS nations:** Accumulating gold, preparing for a post-dollar world. The U.S. BTC reveal would devastate this strategy overnight.
- **EU:** Splits under pressure. No unified response capability.
- **Middle Eastern sovereign funds:** Quietly hedging BTC exposure. Pragmatic, not ideological.
- **IMF & BIS:** Panicking behind closed doors. Institutional inertia prevents meaningful action.

### The inciting event

The U.S. attempts to activate the Satoshi keys to sign a message proving control. The signature fails. Sabotage is confirmed. The keys were swapped years earlier (timestamp suggests 2015, predating U.S. acquisition).

**Consequences:**
- Markets shake, then freeze
- Capital controls loom
- Every faction with a stake in the monetary order mobilises
- Elias, connected to the anomaly through his research, becomes the fall guy

## THEMATIC WORLD RULES — LOCKED

These are the non-negotiable creative rules for how the world operates in the narrative:

1. **No side is fully good.** The U.S. plan is imperial. Decentralisation isn't pure. The saboteur's logic is coherent but their methods are extreme. Moral ambiguity is the default.

2. **Technology is texture, not exposition.** Describe tech through use, not explanation. A key ceremony is a ritual, not a lecture. Mining is physical labour, not a whiteboard diagram.

3. **Surveillance is weather.** Characters live with it, adapt to it, occasionally escape it. It's not a political statement — it's the environment.

4. **Money is a story.** Currencies, reserves, markets — these are collective fictions enforced by power. Characters who understand this have an advantage. Characters who don't are dangerous.

5. **The network is deeper than anyone's understanding.** No single character, faction, or entity fully comprehends what the Bitcoin network is or what it's becoming. This mystery is preserved through the entire narrative.

6. **Code is a character.** Not literally — but code commits, network behaviour, and cryptographic artefacts have presence, weight, and consequence in scenes. They're not background. They act.
