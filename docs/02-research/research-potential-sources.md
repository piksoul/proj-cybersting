## Wooden Books Series (Amy Jones et al.)

The craft reference library (`docs/craft/`) was built from Plot, Character, Narrative, and Literary Devices. These additional titles could add flavour:

### High Relevance

| Title | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Codes & Cyphers** | Directly relevant—cryptography is central to plot. Could inform technical accuracy, puzzle design, and Elias's expertise. |
| **Fractals** | Emergence, complexity theory, self-organizing systems. The thematic core of "what if the network becomes something unexpected?" |
| **Mind Tricks** | Spectacle theory, psychological manipulation, influence operations. Relevant to how factions control narrative and perception. |

### Medium Relevance

| Title | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Ethics** | The moral questions at the heart of the story—who should control money? What's the cost of power? Character moral compasses. |
| **Music / Acoustics** | Breaking subculture, Berlin club scenes, Tresor atmosphere. Sensory texture for key locations. |
| **Proverbs** | Character voice, dialogue patterns, epigraphs. Could inform how different characters express wisdom/cynicism. |

### Low Relevance (Flavour Only)

| Title | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Sacred Geometry / Golden Section** | Could inform graphic novel visual design, or a specific character's worldview/obsession. |
| **Islamic Design / Symmetry** | Visual motifs, pattern language if relevant to settings. |
| **Mazes & Labyrinths** | Metaphorical resonance with the network, trapped characters, no-exit situations. |

### Careful Use (Character Beliefs, Not Narrative Endorsement)

| Title | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Ley Lines** | A character's belief system; conspiracy flavour without narrative endorsement. |
| **Crop Circles** | Red herring potential; character who sees patterns everywhere. |
| **Megalithic Structures / Earth Grids** | Esoteric texture for specific character types. |
| **Ancient Calendars** | Timing obsession; numerology-adjacent character traits. |

---

## How to Use Without Derailing

1. **Character Lens**: Beliefs belong to characters, not the narrative voice
2. **Thematic Resonance**: Embody concepts through story, don't lecture
3. **Homework Fodder**: If acquired, run a homework round exploring applications
4. **World-Building Detail**: Brief references that reward attention without burdening pace
