# CYBERSTING — Strategic Theory Handbook Overview
### research-theory-overview.md

This overview links all three major research theory files used for worldbuilding and narrative consistency.

---

## Handbook Structure

### 1. Geopolitics & Grand Strategy
Explains *why* nations behave as they do.

### 2. Military Strategy & Operational Doctrine
Explains *how* militaries fight and plan.

### 3. Cognitive, Cyber & Information Warfare
Explains *how* minds, networks, and narratives are shaped.

---

## How to Use This Handbook
- Align factions with distinct geopolitical worldviews.  
- Structure military conflict around real doctrines (MDO, A2/AD, RMA).  
- Use cognitive and information warfare for sub-threshold conflict.  
- Maintain consistency by referring to these frameworks in plot logic.
