## Quick Summary

A 90k-110k word techno-thriller where the U.S. government attempts to activate the Satoshi Bitcoin keys to trigger a global monetary reset—only to discover the keys have been sabotaged years ago. A hunted cryptographer races to uncover who really controls the world's most powerful algorithmic asset before a silent financial war turns catastrophic. Tom Clancy geopolitics + Jason Bourne pacing + William Gibson atmosphere + Guy Debord philosophy.
